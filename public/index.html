Segments · geometry and perimeter contracts

Hatched means deferred, red means wall, green means open, and a partial aperture shows its open span. Perimeter segments are adjacency contracts: any defers; open, wall, or a partial aperture filters topology and does not negotiate a seam. The panel below inspects whichever segment was last touched.

Cell classes · the classes any tile may paint

A class is declared once here and then available to every tile. Clutter is intrinsic to the class; loot density is not — it comes from the tier zone a cell sits in.

Topology hints · coarse side seams

Seam classes the tile-edge solver reads while laying out the map. They are layered over the design rather than part of it; what a side actually carries is stated per segment and per vertex. Leave every side on any to state nothing.
