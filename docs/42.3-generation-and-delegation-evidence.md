# 42.3. Generation and delegation evidence

[42](42-performance-history.md) routes the performance record and current slowdown status. These are historical measurements at their stated checkpoints.

## Bundled block fallback reachability (#602 / #535, 2026-10-26)

The bundled-library audit `node tools/block-library-audit.mjs` inventories block
sources in all three resolved libraries. The authored block portions of
`city-block`, `downtown` and `city-centre` are respectively 72 x 24,
120 x 36 and 180 x 48 cells. Each exceeds the SDK's 64-cell axis limit.
The block tiles have zero fill weight, and the libraries have no other
declared block cells or adjacency requests for block. Placement requires every
set-piece slot to fit, and resolution retains declared classes; regions join
four-connected cells of the same class. Thus a block region containing any of
these intact rectangles remains over the limit, even if it merges with another
block. This conclusion applies to these bundled libraries and current placement
and resolution rules, not custom libraries or future content.

The live-recipe search used the default roster, bundled libraries and numeric
room seeds: 24 x 12 seeds 1--44 yielded 119 block briefs and 36 x 18 seed 1
yielded six, with no fallback entries. It extracted macro briefs without building
complete maps; seed 45 was interrupted. These samples corroborate the inventory,
but the content and placement constraints explain the result. The synthetic
fallback witnesses below remain valid for eligible briefs; they do not demonstrate
an effect on current bundled live maps. Wider adoption needs an explicit policy
and cost decision rather than more random seeds.
