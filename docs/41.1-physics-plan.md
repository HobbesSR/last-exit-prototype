## P0: comparison, not two production engines

Build the same seeded scenario/action format for the legacy baseline, bounded
custom prototype and Rapier candidate. The legacy baseline is a timing/control
reference, not expected to pass new dynamic-body cases. Do not build a general
engine abstraction before the witnesses identify needed operations. Prototype
ownership: `tools/physics/`, focused tests and reports; no default simulation change.

Required witnesses:

- Pair head-on, glancing slide, coincident spawn, three-body corner, mixed roles,
  stationary blocker and a dense doorway queue. Track penetration and wall escape.
- Ice acceleration/braking, slow terrain, impulse while steering, pushing a prop,
  resting contact, rotation/long prop and sleeping wake-up.
- Fast bodies through thin walls, moving actor pairs, mask holes and distant map
  coordinates. Validate continuous paths, not just endpoints.
- Spring stretch/damping, zero-distance anchors, rope slack, unequal mass, blocked
  pull, door closure, deletion and two opposing tethers.
- Same-input repeated run, checkpoint/continue versus uninterrupted run, body
  insertion/removal reuse, Node/browser replay of the same *lab* actions.
- Drop/pickup bursts, visibility churn and projected local prediction under network
  delay. Count initialization/disposal and memory growth across repeated worlds.

For #451 remediation, retain zero wall/door/playable-mask escape and finite state
at every substep. Static contact may reach a temporary peak penetration of at
most 0.5 world units, then each affected pair must recover to at most 0.05 within
four ticks (0.2 seconds). Actor and prop penetration caps remain at most 0.5;
rope excess remains at most 1 world unit. These proposed gates replace the
initial static and spring tolerances only for the rerun; the original measurements
and failures remain in 42.6. Passing lab measurements are in 42.9; #472 reviewed
these gates/settings.

Cap each body's speed at `0.8 * min(body minimum radius, 12) /
substepSeconds`: at the contestant reference radius this gives 384/768/1,536
world units/second for 2/4/8 substeps. Larger bodies do not receive a higher
cap. Faster values must be refused before solve and handled by swept query or
explicit teleport. This refusal is a lab admission rule; P1 must enforce the
same invariant after motors, forces and impulses.

Use 24 world units of speculative contact distance (0.6 engine metres at 40
world units/metre); leave other Rapier settings unchanged unless reviewed results
justify a change. Four substeps is the provisional integration setting for this
remediation and is pinned in P1's experimental profile after #472 review.
It does not qualify production capacity or activate default matches.

Replace the previous one-second 5%-of-reference spring gate with initial stretch
15 world units and no growth above 15; the stretch must settle to <=0.5 by 3
seconds and stay <=0.5 through 4 seconds. Evaluate native Rapier center springs
at stiffness/damping pairs 20/4, 2,000/60 and 20,000/200. Reject configurations
that request finite `maxForce` rather than pretending the backend enforces it;
native springs are evaluated uncapped. No hook force-cap/break policy is
approved. Visual trace review is separate from a human-feel playtest. Jelly and
squish remain advanced deferred work under #455.
Identical supported-runtime runs require identical ordered gameplay-state hashes;
cross-runtime and exact checkpoint results are reported separately as supported
or unsupported capabilities, never confused with recorded-state replay.

Provisional capacity envelope, to measure rather than claim as supported: 11
actors (current default), 27 (24 contestants plus 3 gladiators), and 64 stress
actors; 0/128/512 awake props or loot bodies, 0/8/32 tethers, and 128 moving
projectiles. Use sparse full maps and intentionally clustered contacts; sleeping
body totals are measured separately. Record when a workload exceeds a candidate's
supported limit; never silently reduce the load to obtain a pass.

Exercise distant geometry over the supported live-room bounding extents: 17,280
× 8,640, 34,560 × 17,280 and maximum 51,840 × 25,920 world units (12×6, 24×12
and 36×18; 14, 55). Do not use one million units as a supported map-coordinate
target; there is no universal macro playground maximum.

Pin source, package/lockfile and WASM hash, machine, OS, Node/browser, seed,
scenario, geometry, warm-up, duration and solver settings. Sweep two/four/eight
substeps. Run timings sequentially under the project benchmark lock; report
p50/p95/p99/max, allocations/heap, serialization, frame bytes, initialization and
browser frame impact. Store raw runs under `docs/reports/` and findings in a new
42 child per [33](33-maintenance.md). Repeat suspected regressions three times.

Initial *engineering targets*, not measured guarantees: at the selected supported
envelope, physics p95 <= 5 ms and p99 <= 10 ms per 50 ms server tick; whole tick
p99 <= 25 ms on the recorded reference machine, leaving headroom for multiple
rooms. Client prediction p95 <= 2 ms per rendered frame, with a hard replay-work
cap and bounded backlog. Retain existing server/client benchmark comparisons;
the total room count supported must be measured, not derived from physics alone.
Any target change records why and the supported envelope. Correctness/privacy
failures disqualify the selected direction regardless of speed. If Rapier cannot
meet the reviewed gates, escalate and reconsider the reserve Planck.js direction
or advertised scope; do not revive the custom solver implicitly. Record license,
dependency pins, rejected options, failures, maintenance burden and no-go evidence.

## Independent equipment choices

These defaults make lab/implementation work actionable without pretending they
are user-approved balance. Promote only with explicit content definitions and
tests; record deviations in [17.3](17.3-product-gameplay.md) as assumptions, not
verbatim answers.

L0 introduces stable instance identity and quantity conservation first, retaining
current six-slot behavior and controls. Keep map-generated item IDs stable;
allocate deterministic identities when old carried items lack one. Define split,
merge, death, full inventory and failed drop before adding physical bodies.

L1 uses five probability mixes selected by the accepted five virtual columns and
vertical 0/1/2 offset capped at mix 5. Starting weights over qualities 1..5:

| Mix | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | Q5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 70 | 25 | 5 | 0 | 0 |
| 2 | 35 | 45 | 18 | 2 | 0 |
| 3 | 10 | 30 | 40 | 18 | 2 |
| 4 | 0 | 10 | 30 | 45 | 15 |
| 5 | 0 | 0 | 10 | 40 | 50 |

Start with quality affecting weapon damage only at multipliers
1.00/1.08/1.16/1.24/1.32; no implicit knockback/mass or magazine scaling. Novelty
is a separate vertical weight, initially with an empty special-item catalogue;
do not invent special weapons to fill it. Same weapon/quality may refill under
legacy rules until L2 lands; different quality never silently upgrades/downgrades
or merges. Danger reads the same spatial mix selector but keeps independent
curves and safe reservations; trap rearming/concealment F-16 remains separate.
L1 must specify virtual-grid boundary mapping for live masks/legacy diamonds and
document a fallback for arbitrary maps. Test population distributions, not a
monotonic sequence of individual pickups.

L2 starting policy: per-player bounded reserve counters outside the six slots;
three explicit ammo families (pistol/rifle/shell); weapon-owned loaded magazine;
reserve transfers on death/drop through explicit ammo pickup items. Ammo
reserve pickups add directly to the matching counter even with six occupied
slots, taking only available capacity and leaving the remainder in the ground
instance. Full reserves leave it untouched. A living player may drop an explicit
bounded reserve quantity through a validated ammo-drop intent, not by dropping
a loaded weapon (which retains its own magazine). Initial
capacities are pistol 12/72 reserve, rifle 30/120, scattergun 6/30 and reload times
1.0/1.5/1.5 seconds, all content data. These capacities cut totals sharply: today
pistol, rifle and scattergun carry 240, 450 and 120 rounds (`ammo` + `maxAmmo` in
`shared/simulation/content.ts`), against 84, 150 and 36 here. The user will rebalance
ammo (17.3 Q4), so treat these as placeholders, not a chosen balance.
Seed loaded+reserve totals deliberately at spawn; do not multiply existing ammo
by treating all of it as both loaded and reserve. R becomes reload, move/merge moves to M, with drag/touch alternatives
retained and updated help. Movement is allowed during reload; attack is blocked;
switch/drop/death/stun cancels before transfer. On completion transfer exactly
`min(capacity-loaded, reserve)` once, preserving every unused round. No
auto-reload initially. Whole-magazine reload for scattergun first; per-shell
reload is separate. The issue must test input conflicts and explain these
assumptions in the UI/playtest notes rather than burying them in code.

## Acceptance and promotion

Every implementation issue owns focused tests plus its numbered documentation
update. Follow [31](31-verification.md): fast files <15 s, slow files <60 s;
measure grown files using `npm run test:times -- <file>`. Integration runs
`npm run check`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run test:all`,
`npm run test:browser`, `npm run bench`, `npm run bench:client`, and relevant
map/chain gates if generation contracts change. Timings run sequentially.

P9 must verify all three identities separately: old fixture unchanged; new rules
deterministic in the supported runtime; old and new recordings drawable without
the current solver. Replay seek/gaps, delayed spectators, projected hidden
contacts, reconnect, full inventory, charged-cell conservation, bots blocked by
props and room teardown are integration witnesses, not optional polish.

Add debug overlays for body/head/hurt shapes, contact normals, casts, forces,
sleep state and tether anchors, gated to authorized developer views. A small
playtest lab exposes tuning and exports the pinned configuration/action trace.
Human feel tests cover glancing slide, crowd squeeze, impulse recovery and hook
fairness. Automated gates do not establish that these feel good.

Do not turn physics on for default matches before the milestone's projection,
prediction, replay and gameplay gates pass. Pin the selected mode at creation,
retain an explicit legacy mode for characterization, and enable new defaults in
a separate reviewed content/rules checkpoint. No live mid-match fallback.

## Decisions still needing evidence or user preference

Four substeps, 40 units/metre and a <=27 actor/128 awake body/8 tether integration
envelope are proposed from the #451 measurements in 42.9. The 512 awake body
stress scope exceeds the timing budget and is explicitly excluded. These are
synthetic lab bounds, not production capacity guarantees. Head/body ratio, shove
strength, body blocking and input feel need a visual playtest. Hook kit assignment
and numbers, ammo capacities/controls and tier statistics are provisional above;
R as reload is user-confirmed, and ammo capacities await the user's rebalance (17.3 Q4).
Height/roof/projectile interactions and multi-floor traversal need further design
before P7 promotion. None prevents the comparison or item-identity work starting.
