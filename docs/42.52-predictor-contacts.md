## Declared budgets

Each candidate is compared with exact delivery on the same profile, with the
authoritative simulation and input stream kept exact:

- Maximum paired predicted-position distance: at most 2/512 world unit.
- Increase in p99 and maximum pre-correction error: at most 2/512 unit.
- Identical prediction availability and cut reasons at corresponding operations.
- Identical predictor collision-event traces at corresponding new-input and replay
  steps: body pair, start/stop type and substep. Zero changed contact-event steps.
- Rounding error at most half of each candidate quantum from 24.1.
- An exact repeat must reproduce poses, cuts and contact events exactly.

A failing profile fails the candidate; results are not averaged across profiles.
Coverage must demonstrate actual predictor contact with rotated solid props and
moving actor proxies, generated geometry and nonzero candidate perturbations.
An unexercised field is unqualified. These are conservative workload bounds,
not a proof for arbitrary geometry or network conditions.

## Finding

**Keep collision-proxy and own-body pose quantization off.** Both exceed the
position budget against exact delivery in the rotated-prop and moving-actor
profiles. The wider, all-fields candidate also fails. Matching contact event
identities alone does not imply matching predicted motion.

Linear velocity alone met all declared budgets and field-coverage gates in
the original arrival-order workload below. The stamped-input rerun under #1096
exceeds its position budget with the moving actor (see the follow-up below), so
that earlier qualification does not carry forward. The original result was evidence
for a separately reviewable candidate, not an
activation decision. Proxy velocities are not consumed by the current predictor;
the meaningful solver comparison here is the own body's initial vx/vy.
[24.3](24.3-wire-data-model.md) identifies an exact float32-metre representation
for body velocities. Any future velocity quantization decision must justify
bandwidth savings against that exact representation; this witness measures no
such savings. No change to 24.2 decision 3 is made.

## Workload and coverage

`tools/physics/prediction-contacts.mjs` runs the real `content-7` game and ten
production predictors per profile: exact, identical exact repeat, the three
ownership groups, four numeric families, and all candidates together. Only the
frames delivered to candidate predictors are rounded. Shapes, motor tuning,
authority, inputs, gates and item bodies stay exact. Each profile runs 80 ticks
at 20 Hz. Position and cut state are compared after every input/correction call;
the opt-in observer captures each new-input and replay solver step after the
production contact drain and publication of the completed pose and tick.

- **Rotated prop:** an authored crate begins at 0.35 radians and moves/rotates
  under contact. Exact delivery produces 68 collision events, including 66 in
  replay, across 233 predictor steps (156 replay). Assertions require actual
  `proxy:prop:rotated` contact, replay contact and nonzero transmitted-angle
  perturbations for that prop.
- **Moving actor:** a second contestant moves toward/across the local player's
  path. Exact delivery produces 47 events, including 44 in replay. Assertions
  require changing transmitted positions and actual `proxy:actor:c1` contact
  during prediction and replay. The predictor holds each proxy fixed during
  its replay horizon, as production does.
- **Generated map:** the stored seed-9 12x6 room arena is read unchanged. The
  local actor contacts generated `obstacle:o1367`, with 66 events including 65
  in replay. An assertion requires static-obstacle contact. This is generated
  geometry, not a newly generated map or a replacement fixture.
- **Delivery/fallbacks:** nominal RTT 0/100/200 ms, an application frame gap,
  stale authority, placement, generation change and tethered fallback. The last
  three markers are injected into production projections at tick 22; the
  witness asserts the expected cut there. The stale profile withholds both
  inputs and frames long enough for the next input to cut stale prediction.

Nonzero perturbation coverage is combined across profiles; every profile must
still meet every budget. Per-profile `coverageComplete` records local coverage,
while the report's summary combines it across the workload. An unperturbed field
does not become qualified because another field in its group was exercised.

The witness asserts deterministic controls and required scenario coverage. It

suite. No timing comparison or performance claim is made.

## Limits

This measures Node production prediction with synthetic, ordered delivery. It
does not measure sockets, datagram loss, bandwidth, browser scheduling or the
older witness's jitter, reconnect, background, hidden-contact, stale-door and
burst profiles. The generated-map profile covers one path on one stored map.
Its collision is with an axis-aligned static obstacle; the rotated-prop and
moving-actor failures come from the authored scenes.

Contacts here are collision start/stop facts, with body identity and substep.
Continuous contact manifolds, normals, separation and impulses are not compared;
identical event traces cannot establish their equality. The position failures
already reject wider pose rounding under the declared gates.

Own heading, body angle and body omega remain zero in this workload. Heading
and omega are not consumed by the current local motor solve; own body angle
is supplied to its circular collider. Proxy velocity and omega are delivered
but omitted when production constructs fixed proxies. Passing a no-effect field
cannot establish future predictor behavior that consumes it.
