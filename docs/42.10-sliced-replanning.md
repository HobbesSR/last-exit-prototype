# 42.10. Sliced replanning, #470 part 1

Status: measured 2026-10-23. Up: [42](42-performance-history.md). Design:
[22.6](22.6-hierarchical-navigation.md#bot-adoption-stage-4), "Sliced replanning". Follows
[42.8](42.8-bot-adoption.md).

## Result

Under `content-4` a bot's repath is one search of up to 100,000 expansions, spread over ticks,
and all bots share 3,000 expansions a tick. Across the 13 bot-only matches of 42.8, no plan ran
out of budget: `content-3`, at 12,000 expansions a search, ran out 102 times at 24 × 12 and 182
times at 36 × 18 (none at 12 × 6). Escapes, stalls and doors opened are unchanged within a match
or two. Searches finish in 1.1 to 1.6 slices on average, so a bot waits a tick or two for its route.

The cost is a little more search: bots now finish the searches they used to cut off. At 36 × 18
the mean tick rose 5% and p95 by about 1.5 ms. The worst ticks are no higher, because the shared
allowance caps a tick's search at 3,000 expansions where `content-3` spent up to 12,000 at once.

**For #425:** the expansion budget no longer binds on these maps once a search may span ticks, so
the coarse level of #470 part 3, which #425 would feed, is not needed for budget. #425 stays
deferred (22.6, "Implementation sequence").

## Why the allowance is shared

The first version gave each bot its own 2,000 expansions a tick. Measured cold, as 42.8 was, it
also exhausted nothing, but at 36 × 18 its mean tick was 10.4 ms against `content-3`'s 6.9, p95
19.3 against 11.3, and 26 ticks over 50 ms against 10. Counting expansions per tick on seed 1 at
36 × 18 showed why: total work rose only 16% (1.65 million expansions in 6,000 ticks against 1.42
million), but bots' searches coincided, up to 6,257 expansions on one tick and 237 ticks between
4,000 and 8,000. A shared allowance of 3,000 a tick, taken in player order, capped every tick at
3,000 with total work unchanged. The cold run is kept as
[reports/navigation-470-unpooled-cold.json](reports/navigation-470-unpooled-cold.json).

## Limits

- **Few matches.** 13 matches over 13 maps, as in 42.8. Zero exhaustion is shown on these maps,
  not established for every map at 36 × 18.
- **Route latency.** A bot whose search takes several slices walks its old route, or straight at
  its target when it has none, meanwhile. Reaction to a nearby change waits for the slices; #470
  part 2 (repair nearby changes first) addresses that.
- **Starvation.** The allowance is taken in player order, so when searches pile up, bots late in
  the list wait longest. With 1.1 to 1.6 slices per search this was not seen to matter.
