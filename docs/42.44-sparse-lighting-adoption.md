# 42.44. The sparse-sides and regional-lighting adoption batch (#895)

Back to [42](42-performance-history.md). One content batch under 31's rule ("Content changes: one
bump per batch"): #851's sparser tile sides, scheme `sparse / alternate`
([42.40](42.40-sparse-sides-experiment.md)), and #878's default regional lighting
([42.43](42.43-regional-lighting.md)), with one `REGION_TYPES_VERSION` bump (`types-67`) and one
chain-baseline recapture. Corey's decision is recorded on #851 (2026-11-09). The connectivity
skeleton (#883, [42.42](42.42-connectivity-skeleton-experiment.md)) is not in the batch: it stays
a Map Lab World option, switched off, until after the Foundation Pass (#910).

## What changed

- **Libraries.** `common-primitives@7`, `common-set-pieces@7` and `district-set-pieces@6` bake in
  `sparseSidesLibrary(…, { fill: 'sparse', setPieces: 'alternate' })` over `@6`/`@5`; the three
  diamonds pin them, carry their own set pieces' rewritten slots (one tile and tile set at 12 and
  24 wide, six at 36), and state `fitPlacedSetPieces: true` (#856). 52 lists the content.
- **Equivalence.** For each authored size, the resolved library equals what the retired function
  built from the old modules: the same fill tiles (ids, weights, segments, in order), the same
  slot contents (each slot's members as tile designs), the same classes and settings. Only the new
  tile-set ids (`<set>-sp-<n>`) are numbered differently, so maps differ by seed from the Lab's
  experiment but not in kind (the macro panel below).
- **Retired.** `map/sparse-sides.ts`, the Map Lab's "Bundled, sparser tile sides" library source
  and its fill and set-piece menus, and `library-comparison.mts --sparse`: applying the scheme on
  top of the adopted content would be wrong. The `face` and `inner` schemes can be rebuilt from
  that module in git history before #895, applied to the `@6`/`@5` modules.
- **Lighting** (#878, Codex's branch merged in without rebase): `stampBuiltMap` always appends
  the regional fields; the opt-in is gone. Presentation only.

## Pins that moved

Content pins, moved with the content (31):
- `map-sparse-sides` now checks the bundled libraries for the experiment's invariants: valid, no
  set-piece seam problems, `fitPlacedSetPieces` stated, every side as matched (`@6`) or hinted
  whole, sparse families present, inner half-and-half seams hinted, an outer opening kept, and
  pieces placed side by side fitting (1.7 s alone; it was a Lab-scheme test before).
- `map-authored-seams` and `map-hall-wing-library`: an exposed half-and-half side may be hinted
  whole, and a piece keeps a non-empty subset of its original external portals.
- `map-hall-wing-library` checks portal reach thoroughly, as the map diagnosis does: on the winged
  hall's L brief the grid search reports one unreachable portal on seeds 1, 7 and 533, and the
  thorough search finds every portal reachable (the same false negative as #849's rubble).
- `map-clear-tiles` and `map-hall-wing-library` read a slot's authored set as the stem of its
  `-sp-<n>` set; the inward-source helper points the diamonds' local slots back the same way
  (checked to reproduce the old local set pieces exactly).
- `map-lab-routes` (route lengths, ratio 1.086 → 1.095) and `slow/view-live-map` (last obstacle,
  door and item ids on seeds 1 and 2).
- the Map Lab browser check's hut drill-down finds the first `last-exit-NNN` seed whose briefs
  hold a hut (macro alone) instead of pinning `last-exit-006`, which no longer places one.
- `map-skeleton` builds on the bundled library where it built on the Lab's `sparse / alternate`
  or `sparse / face`; zero sealed-pocket refusals still holds at 12 × 6.

Every corridor and portal query completed. The 36 × 18 maps' whole-map searches exhausted their
400,000-expansion budget on 8–18 trips on both sides, which leaves those trips out.

**The worst ratio grows materially.** `main` had already moved past 42.22's figures (median
1.002–1.015, worst 1.081) to medians of 1.029–1.059 and a worst of 1.277. The batch takes medians
to 1.047–1.170 and the worst to 2.354. The likely reason, not separately measured: with fewer
portals per region, a straight line between crossings says less about the walk between them. By
31's rule the upgrade path is lazily learned crossing lengths. Hunter behaviour is currently
parked, so this is recorded for that work, not fixed here: deferred issue #913. This measures bot
planning against the true shortest walk, not the map's own detour, which rose as intended (42.40).

## Set pieces side by side (#856)

`placement-probe.mts --size 12 --prefix sweep --from 1 --to 30`, #856's acceptance probe: all 30
seeds placed in 121 attempts, 91 of which failed at the set-piece stage (`landing` 53, `evac` 30,
`transit-station` 8) and **none in the solve**. #856's zero-refusal failures at the solve's first
step are gone. A misfit is now refused when the piece is placed, which is cheaper still (41.9 s for
the 30 seeds, worst 2.3 s). With 42.41's off-by-default `--start-end-first`, the same seeds take 42
attempts (11 set-piece failures, one solve failure), evidence for #891's ordering, not changed here.
