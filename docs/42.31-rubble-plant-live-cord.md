# 42.31. Rubble and plant on a live cord

Back to [42](42-performance-history.md). Issue #759 under #529, following park's move in
[42.29](42.29-park-perturbable-routes.md). Direction: [17.2.11](17.2.11-navigation-structure-direction.md#route-protection-is-too-strong).
Content version `types-62`, which also adopts park's hedge fronts (#760, measured in
[42.30](42.30-park-hedge-fronts.md)). Builder mechanics are in [54.1](54.1-ground-and-friction.md#rubble)
and [20.3.4](20.3.4-structure-builders.md#plant-builder), the coordinator in [20.10](20.10-portal-cords.md).

## Change

Rubble and plant no longer reserve `protectedRoutes` between portals. As park did in #750,
they keep only the portal approach strips (`portalApproaches`) and place through
`createProspectiveParcels`, which refuses a commit that would leave no hunter cord from the first
stand to every portal stand. A refused piece is passed over. Plant does this up to 32 portals and
keeps its boundary aisle past that, as before. Both pass `SCATTER_CORD_BUDGET`, the 16,384 witness
path segments park's fronts already used (20.10). The coordinator's default of 4,096 is unchanged:
the sweep replay below found a 29-portal rubble region whose first cord needed more.

The two were chosen as representatives of 42.29's two kinds of reuse:

- **Rubble** is the most numerous type and had the highest route share (67% of fill, 42.28). Its
  hint walls read the routes as `avoid`. They now avoid only the approaches and its own debris,
  and `commitHintWalls` can pass over: if the cord refuses the walls as one assembly, they commit
  a wall at a time and each refused wall is left out.
- **Plant** is a large, many-portal type (56% of fill in routes), so it tests the cost 42.29
  expected there. Its siting already read only the canvas. The shared infill helper now takes any
  siting canvas, reads the coordinator's addressed record again after each commit, and passes
  over a refused crate when the builder gives no `commitError`.

## Method

`node tools/park-route-audit.mjs --type rubble` and `--type plant`: 42.29's method, panel and
measures unchanged. One map per size, `size-{12,24,36}-1`, from `generate(seed, params, undefined, 48)`
with #688's parameters. "Before" rebuilds the type's briefs with main's strategy (`3fbd082`),
whose frozen routes are the only difference, and each side holds the other type already moved.
Plant's audit ran before fronts were adopted (types-61 parks on both sides, digests pinned at
`d221cb5`); rubble's ran after the shared budget below, with types-62 parks on both sides (`876c38a`).
The retained `summary.json` and `examples.json.gz` are in [reports/issue-759-rubble](reports/issue-759-rubble/)
and [reports/issue-759-plant](reports/issue-759-plant/), with their source digests.

Proofs passed on every changed region: `validateFillConstraints`, a thorough `validatePortalReach`
and a rebuild with reversed cell and zone order. Both maps of each size passed `checkMap` with
diagnosis, and every final spawn → exit and spawn → charger target completed for both bodies.

## Before rolling out further

#759 asked to stop and reassess here. The other five strategies are cover, ruins, depot, hall and
market.

- **The effect is known.** Three types now show the same thing: freeing routes about doubles
  content and barely changes detour or dead-end area. A further rollout would repeat that.
- **Cost is the constraint.** Each move costs roughly ten times the builder's old time, and a
  36-wide map now takes 51 s rather than 31 s. Depot, market and hall are large, many-portal types
  like plant. The next exact saving 42.29 names is an incremental mesh, rather than a rebuild when
  a piece meets a cord.
- **The other readers still need work.** Cover's `sitedCover` protections feed `coverAround`,
  `coverNear` and the delegation receiver. Ruins' and hall's hint walls can now pass over,
  as rubble's do.
