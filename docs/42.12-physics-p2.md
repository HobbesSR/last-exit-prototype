retain `last-exit-0.7`/`content-4`; omitted physics-factory content retains P1.

finite, insertion-independent trajectory is asserted, not immediate recovery
from arbitrary overlap. Normal placement refuses or relocates those spawns.

unchanged projectile hit envelopes beyond the smaller bodies, immutable tuning,
surface priority and stable-ID ties, low traction/braking, slowed movement,
bounded impulse recovery and actual-path distance, speed/impulse admission,
reversed rosters, occupied gates, direct door interactions including props,
teleport/joint/velocity/generation cleanup, death, impossible spawn and deferred
respawn. A blocked transit preserves cooldown/effects; a valid landing clears
motion and constraints. Between-tick teleport markers survive the next completed
capture and then expire. These are intentional new expectations, separate from
the frozen fixture.

## Verification and limitations

Integration verification results and sequential timing samples are recorded
below after the final gates complete.

Initial browser integration exposed an older direct scene fixture without the
new callback. The callback is now optional, retaining old scenes; the focused
overhead browser check passed after correction. Placement tests also caught
stale native collider positions after teleport; occupancy queries now propagate
changed body positions before testing. Integration review caught a rejected
transit's spent cooldown, which now has a preflight placement guard and test.

This is an experimental character slice. P3/P4 still own physical projections,
recorded-state presentation and prediction, P8 owns navigation and general
dynamic geometry, and P9 owns promotion and human-feel qualification. Passing
automated witnesses does not establish pleasant sliding, crowd squeeze or
impulse feel. The historical large-body-count failure and reported slowdown
remain unresolved. No default activation or exact backend restore is claimed.
