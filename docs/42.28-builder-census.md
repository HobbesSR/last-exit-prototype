# 42.28. Builder census

Back to [42](42-performance-history.md). Issue #749 under #529, following
[42.27](42.27-empty-region-fallback.md) (#717). The goals it measures against are in
[17.2.11](17.2.11-navigation-structure-direction.md); types are in [54](54-region-types.md)
and cords in [20.10](20.10-portal-cords.md). Content version `types-59`.

## Method

Panel: the nine #716/#717 maps, `size-{12,24,36}-{1,2,3}` from `generate(seed, params, undefined, 48)`
with #688's parameters. That is 10,333 regions and about 1.42 million cells. Every brief is
counted as `generate` built it. Nothing is navigated, checked or timed.

- **Empty**: no collision geometry, as in 42.27. An empty cover, park or rubble region is
  rebuilt once at density 1. If something then fits, the draw declined; if not, nothing fits.
- **Margin**: the share of owned cells that are not fill (clear margins, 20.6).
- **Covered**: the share of fill inside obstacle shapes, sampled at 16 points a cell. A
  building counts its walls, not its interior.
- **Routes**: the share of fill inside `protectedRoutes(brief)`, using the mode and portal
  limit each strategy uses. Only the eight strategies that call it have a value. Obstacles
  never overlap these shapes, so covered plus routes is at most the whole fill.
- **Mechanisms**: manifest counters, then element-label families (delegated children show
  as `delegate:`, block lots as `lot:`). There are two observers where those can't tell.
  `sitedCover` is now exported, so cover's lattice can be told from its #717 fallback (the
  labels are identical). Hut and compound's existing `observeYard` gives yard handoff
  outcomes and refusal reasons.

Reproduce with `node tools/builder-census.mjs`. A cold run generates the panel into
`test-results/issue-749/` in about 2¼ minutes. The census itself takes about 80 seconds.
The retained `summary.json` is in [reports/issue-749](reports/issue-749/), with pinned
source digests and every count below.

## Candidates

These are findings, not proposals. Each would be its own issue.

1. **Route protection takes most of the fill where it applies.** In root mode it holds
   56–67% of fill in rubble, cover, plant, hall and ruins, and 37% in park. Depot and market
   use `'nearby'` mode and lose 8.6%, but their regions are larger, so this is not a
   controlled comparison. That is the 17.2.11 "route protection is too strong" finding,
   now measured. Obstacles cover at most 11% of any type's fill.
2. **Cover's lattice almost never fires.** Only 53 of 1,030 regions (5%) get lattice
   clusters. The #717 fallback is cover's working builder: the median cover region is one
   tile (20 fill cells), smaller than the 6-cell period after margins and routes.
3. **Park's own content is rare.** Hedges, clumps or a pocket appear in 62 of 1,449 parks
   (4%), pockets in 19. The fallback places trees in 409. In 759 parks (52%) not even one
   tree fits. Most parks are assigned to one-tile regions.
4. **Rubble is mostly margin and route.** Its median fill is 8 cells. Margins take 67% of
   its cells and protected routes take 67% of what is left. 1,784 regions (38%) hold nothing
   even at density 1, and content covers 2.4% of fill.
5. **Clear margins dominate small regions.** In open, rubble, cover, hut and the core types,
   37–67% of owned cells are margin, against 6–11% in the large themed types.
6. **Open is the largest type.** It holds 28% of panel cells in 2,553 regions, by intent
   (M25). Under 17.2.11's "much less empty space", the open share is a macro lever, not a
   builder one.
7. **Mechanisms that rarely fire:** hall infill crates (1 of 41), plant sheds and designed
   buildings (5 of 50), plant pipes (8 of 50), hall wings (10 of 41), park pockets (19 of
   1,449).
8. **Yard handoffs refuse more than they accept.** Compound's cord witness refuses 561
   probes as `external` (the handoff would cut an external portal's access) against 104
   accepted. 139 of 241 huts have no eligible yard, and in 150 hut probes the cover receiver
   placed nothing. The probes' cost was not timed here.
9. **No `unfilledRegion` exit fired on the panel**, in any type. Rubble's empties are
   placement failures inside its full routine, not its early exit.
