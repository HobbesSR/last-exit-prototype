## Witness results

| Case | Observed result |
| --- | --- |
| Low versus tall cover | Same throw clears height-20 cover; height-80 cover stops it at x=58, two units before the wall |
| Height crossing within a tick | Falling cube enters a thin slab during planar overlap; both tick endpoints miss the slab |
| Landing / bounce | Exhausted bounce budget lands; available bounce integrates the remaining tick before later landing |
| Fuse at ground | Fuse wins over bounce/landing; no second outcome |
| Impact/fuse/ground tie | Impact wins; no fuse or ground event is emitted |
| Decorative roof | Identical to the unobstructed scene; roof art supplies no physical ceiling |
| Explicit ceiling | Cube hits the authored underside interval while rising |
| Window opening / sill / lintel | Middle throw clears the authored opening; lower and higher throws hit their respective solids |
| Mask hole / outer edge | Fast high throws stop at expanded hole x=38 and inset outer edge x=198, never crossing void |

Additional unit witnesses check apex tangency, descent onto a slab after planar
entry, earliest-event precedence, stable ID and mask ties, a bounce then fuse in
one tick, no repeated terminal event, invalid/missing heights and finite resource

## Scope and qualification limits

One stationary-box scene and one cube at a time; no moving targets, rotating
objects, circles, dynamic props or arbitrary authored cell-mask geometry. There
is no damage, inventory transfer, multiplayer authority or live network projection.
Bounds are a rectangle with rectangular holes and one named flat ground plane.
All dimensions, gravity, bounce and fuse settings are provisional lab values.
No physical roof or window behavior is inferred from legacy maps. No game
runtime, live content, production replay schema or frozen fixture was modified.
The lab needs no new shared types; its records remain isolated under `tools/physics`.

Height authoring, physical roof/window/material rules, floors, outcomes and version
and presentation/privacy contracts need review before a separately scoped live
integration. P7 remains outside planar-release blockers. No performance or human
feel claim is made by these functional witnesses.
