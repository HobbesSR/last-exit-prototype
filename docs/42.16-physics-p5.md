## Workload and provenance

The [retained report](reports/physics-445-p5-merged.json) was generated with
`node --expose-gc tools/physics/p5-report.mjs`: seed 445, 40 warmup ticks and
200 measured ticks per scene, on Windows 10.0.26200, Node 24.15.0 and an AMD
Ryzen 9 5900X. It pins source commit `8414d67`, clean tracked source, individual
source/package/lock/WASM hashes, Rapier 0.21.0 and full settings/content. The
2400 by 1200 authored map contains eleven stationary non-bot actors. Sparse and
clustered variants contain 0/64/117 loot, 116 loot plus one crate, or 53 loot plus
64 crates. Maximum count is 128 dynamic bodies including the actors. Authoring
is non-overlapping; the report records crate grid positions and map digests.

Each scene runs settling/sleeping and awake conditions. Awake loot gets alternating
+/-2 world-unit x impulses; the 64-crate grid gets +/-20 (sparse) or +/-40 (clustered),
alternating by tick and body index. Impulse preparation is measured separately,
outside the timed step. The final awake scenes retain all 128 bodies awake;
settled scenes retain 117 sleeping non-actor bodies and eleven awake actors.
GC runs twice between disposed scenes, outside timed operations. The benchmark
lock was held; this worktree's tests/browser/chain processes had finished.
The earlier uncollected smoke report is retained locally at
`test-results/p5-report-pre-gc.json`; the checked-in report is a separate GC-instrumented run.

Witnesses cover static-only loot contacts; pickup/drop/death quantity and charge;
atomic failed admission and E swaps, including full batch reservation ahead of
logical overflow; sleeping pickup, lifecycle markers and joint cleanup; forty
physical transfers with bounded body/joint/pending counts; generation rejection;
stable deterministic traces; floor priority/ID selection; crate pushing/rotation
without contact damage; mask holes, thin walls, maximum supported map corners
and crowd pressure; independent shield/damage/impulse, static blast cover/falloff;
map authoring privacy; item-layer motion and engine-free backward playback.

No default activation, procedural crate/floor generation, movable sight/blast cover,
crush/breakable chains or hook ability is introduced. Full P8 navigation and P9
promotion/human-feel/production capacity qualification remain required.
