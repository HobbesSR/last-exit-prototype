# 42.24. Connected static-barrier experiment (#694)

This bounded experiment under #529 follows the direction in
[17.2.11](17.2.11-navigation-structure-direction.md) and the baseline in
[42.23](42.23-navigation-structure.md). It adds one fixed connected bottle wall
to a copy of each retained map, keeping production generation, compatibility
versions, fixtures, libraries, original geometry and targets unchanged.
The runnable preview, exact inputs/sources, full reports, failure counts and
reproduction commands are retained in [the report](reports/issue-694/README.md).

The pattern is an interior seed with a single mouth, not a wall attached to a
region boundary. Its five touching rectangles make a 14×14-cell enclosure with
one-cell walls and a 2.5-cell opening. It demonstrates both a target-relative
barrier and a substantial accessible pocket without prospective parcel growth
or movable cords. This is a geometric witness, not an authorship theme or a
player exploration study.

## Matched comparison

The nine pocket-target pairs are deliberately selected on opposite sides of
the west wall, at three depths and three offsets. Three west-to-east external
crossings and three mouth-approach controls distinguish external deflection from
entering the pocket. Gameplay and across-map endpoints come unchanged from
#688's before reports, retaining body and distance stratum. All matched paths
use whole-map meshes in both conditions, permitting outside bypasses. Endpoints
are never replaced when after geometry blocks them. Descriptive after-report
pair sampling is excluded from matched comparisons.

The pocket, empty-space and boundary measures retain
[51.10](51.10-measurement.md)'s exact definitions. Navigation uses live contestant
and hunter clearances (14 and 25 world units); independent final owner validation
and pocket access use the SDK's larger 0.925-cell hunter. Every affected owner
gets thorough portal validation, while complete before/after maps get the
existing composition, proof and core report. Unchanged owners are not all
diagnosed again (the report README records the stopped broad trial).
These checks establish swept route
witnesses, not universal connectivity or shortest paths.

Measured 2026-10-30 on the exact `size-{12,24,36}-1` saved inputs. Each body has
nine pocket-target routes per map: every before ratio is 1 and every after ratio
exceeds 1.5, with no invalid endpoint, unreachable target, search miss or swept
defect in those 54 after witnesses.

External crossing medians rise from 1 to 1.312 (contestant) and 1.343 (hunter).
The three mouth controls per map/body remain near straight (medians 1.0001 and
1.0020). Each owner gains 132.95 square cells of contestant pocket area and
122.96 for hunter, from zero. Whole-map component counts stay unchanged:
1/1, 2/3 and 2/2 for contestant/hunter at increasing size. All other failures
are the blocked loot endpoints above: no unreachable, search-miss or swept-path
defect is recorded in the fixed comparisons.

All 1,182 retained gameplay/across-map controls complete (203/203 per body at
12×6; 201/201 at 24×12; 185 contestant and 189 hunter at 36×18). For the 24 long
across-map routes per map/body, medians stay in the 1.018–1.024 range; no ratio
exceeds 1.5. Some paths and quantiles change with the mesh, including decreases;
these are search witnesses, not a claim that adding walls shortens true geodesics.
This local experiment does not deliver large-scale progression.

The affected owners' contestant empty shares change 99.76→99.49%,
99.43→99.19% and 97.81→97.37%; hunter shares stay 100%. Each owner's absolute
empty-area estimate falls by 101 square cells per body, but the walkable-sample
denominator also shrinks. This is not a large reduction in empty share or a
solution to the whole empty-space goal. Physical boundary impassability remains
exactly unchanged (owner shares 7.64%, 13.54%, 26.52%); portal counts, ownership,
guaranteed boundary structure and one-portal shares remain exact.

## Integration limit and decision

**Retain the connected static pattern as a mechanics witness; adapt its
composition order before considering production adoption.** A cheap static
composition can provide target-relative detour and an accessible pocket, so a
general growth helper is not a prerequisite for exercising these mechanics.
This does not establish static geometry as the main map-wide lever.

The overlay deliberately preserves every original loot candidate. Some lie in
new walls and become invalid endpoints, despite the portal checks passing. This
is a concrete integration failure: a portal promise is not a pickup-access
guarantee. The experiment reports every retained candidate near the wall rather
than moving targets or sampling only survivors. Production use would need static
authorship before loot siting and access checks for the resulting target set,
with its own explicit content/version/expectation scope. The experiment does not
make that production change.

Local witness success does not imply useful map-scale progression. Only one
owner changes per map; the original endpoints are a bounded sample, and geometry
alone does not force players to enter a pocket. Boundary/portal control remains
a candidate for a separate map-scale experiment. Optional #649 growth remains
another candidate when variable-mask coverage warrants it. Neither follows
automatically from this delivery; do not resume density tuning or stand-in
grammar on the strength of this witness.
