# 42.60. A live player's whole fast frame on generated maps (#1095)

Up: [42](42-performance-history.md). Design: [24.5](24.5-transport.md) "Stage 3's contracts" (Delivery) and
[24.2](24.2-lossy-transport-deltas.md) "Byte budget and overflow". It answers what [42.59](42.59-split-delivery-under-loss.md)
"Limits" left open: no live player's whole fast frame had been measured, so its size against the edge's 1,100-byte
fragment was still 24.2's estimate of 0.66–2.4 KB. Recovery and joins send whole frames.

## Finding

On every live size, a whole fast frame fit one fragment throughout a full match. The largest was 920 B, on 24x12,
and none of 261,151 frames needed a second fragment. 24.2's 0.66–2.4 KB was high: the median whole frame is 281–429 B
and the 99th percentile 603–779 B. Recovery therefore costs one datagram, and the four-fragment allowance is headroom.
Steady deltas against the previous tick had a median of 49–68 B, a 95th percentile of 108–147 B and a maximum of
589 B.

## Results

Seed 9, the shipped content and roster (11 seats), every seat played by a bot, each match played to its end,
2026-11-19. Bytes are the encoded `fast` message before the edge's 4-byte fragment header. "Whole" is the fast part
through the view's 32-tick event window; "delta" is that frame against the previous tick's.

The largest frame on each size:
- **12x6:** 846 B, with 19 bodies, 7 players and 4 events in view.
- **24x12:** 920 B, with 22 bodies, 5 players and 6 events.
- **36x18:** 654 B, with 10 bodies, 10 players and 6 events.

Larger maps spread the same roster thinner, so a view holds less and the median falls with size. A frame's size
follows what is in view, not the map's size.

## Method

`tools/perf/fast-whole-1095.mjs` runs a room on the fake clock, as `tests/helpers/room-sizes.js` does: the owner
starts the match and leaves, so every seat is a bot's. Each tick it takes `room.match.snapshot()`, projects every
seat's player view, and builds that view's fast frame as `deliverSplit` does (`fastFrame`, then one
`createEventWindow` per view, fed every tick). It then encodes a whole `fast` message and a delta against the
previous tick's frame. Waiting views are skipped. It measures bytes only, so it takes no machine lock. Run
`node tools/perf/fast-whole-1095.mjs --sizes=12x6,24x12,36x18`, one size per process: 36x18 took 131 s.

## Limits

- **One seed and the shipped roster.** A bigger roster, or content that puts more bodies in view, grows the
  frame. 24.2's "a bigger roster or map" case is still a projection, now from these sizes.
- **Bots' views.** A bot's view is a player view projected by the same code. Bots may still cluster differently
  from people.
- **Clean-path deltas.** Each delta is against the previous tick. Under loss a delta spans more ticks, and 42.59
  measured those through the edge (at most 245 B).
- **Event bursts.** The largest frames carried at most six events. 24.5 "Events" gives the window's own ceiling,
  past about 115 events.
- **A dated record, not a check.**
