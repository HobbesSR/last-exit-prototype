## Decision and current checkpoint

Build one convincing retail/service-street slice before distributing new art
across the generator. Signature night is the intended production look. Keep
`prototype` as the default until the slice passes the promotion gate below;
the remaining themes stay useful comparison studies. No further aesthetic
selection from Corey is required to start these tasks.

#582 implements the swatches, global themes and live restyling (carried in PR #591). Preserve those
controls as a reference mode. Its scene identities do not yet implement spatial
composition, district metadata, material history or virtual lighting.

The review found a verification gap: the original color test credited special
obstacles with a generic outline that several drawing branches never emit.
The corrected guard covers ordinary outlined rectangles and road/ground colors;
it does not certify every obstacle, actor or hazard. In particular, translucent
glass, tree trunks/crowns, rocks, pipe highlights and partial ruin edges require
rendered witnesses. Do not tune dozens of colors merely to satisfy a fictitious
outline or treat a 1.5 color ratio as perceptual/accessibility certification.
The initial fill-only comparison candidates and their limits are recorded on #587.

## The first slice

Use a compact corner shop or service frontage beside a loading yard: one street,
one exterior opening with its existing threshold treatment, one interior, a
roof, opaque cover, a fence, a tree and glass. Reuse authoritative shape records.
Place a contestant and each hunter identity alongside visible loot, an objective,
and active/inactive hazard examples; hidden examples must remain hidden.

Start with slate paving, warm masonry, a broad legacy-teal painted rooftop,
weathered coral awnings and ochre parking stripes. These are Corey's large-color-field
commercial-block example from [11.1](11.1-world-and-art-direction.md); demonstrate
both roof-covered and interior-revealed views so the landmark survives the fade.
The theatrical treatment is one broad cobalt intersection area with one warm edge/focal area.
A faded panel, a drain stain and a single scorch group must read as different
layers. Add neither random decorative rubble nor simulated structural damage.
This is a renderer witness, not an unreviewed new production building or map seed.

Review at actual `playerZoom`, `INSIDE_ZOOM`, and both sides of the existing
LOD cutoffs from [25](25-pacing-and-rendering.md). Use the same camera, viewport,
device pixel ratio, simulation frame and roof-fade state for comparisons. Include
a short motion pass: static screenshots cannot establish moving-actor recognition.

## Promotion gate and evidence

Record the commit, fixture/seed, theme, viewport, pixel ratio, camera/zoom, fog and
roof state beside captures. Keep reference/candidate captures paired. Use a small
contact sheet for review, with original gameplay-scale images retained; a close-up
concept image is not evidence. Review grayscale and color-vision simulations as
additional checks, not as a substitute for ordinary color/motion inspection.

- **Readability:** demonstrate all special obstacle branches in step 1, visible
  actor identities and threats against both quiet and saturated backgrounds.
  Door/window openings and narrow passages remain recognizable. Decorative wear
  cannot be mistaken for blocking rubble; theatrical color cannot be mistaken for
  a hazard boundary or objective. Architectural graphics must explain former use
  without requiring symbol decoding; contiguous material fields remain legible
  after wear and at gameplay scale. Log remaining ambiguity as a failed gate.
- **State and privacy:** test palette changes during roof fades, camera movement,
  view changes and replay seeking. Remembered gates, discovery, actor containers
  and fade progress survive restyling. Appearance must never reveal a withheld
  actor, trap, item or event, even through a light or shadow.
- **Compatibility:** changes confined to presentation must leave frozen behavior
  fixtures and map geometry unchanged. Any serialized appearance field requires
  its own 53/26 compatibility decision; no opportunistic replay or map bump.
- **Performance:** use 31's relevant browser checks and `npm run bench:client` on
  the same scene/viewport before and after, sequentially without competing work.
  Compare `render.draw`, frame p95/hitches, tile-build cost/count, retained textures
  and memory across first view, pan/zoom, restyle and steady motion. Retain `BUILD_MS`
  and view-bounded caches. Repeat suspected regressions three times. Do not claim
  lighting is cheap merely because its colors were calculated once.

Promotion requires resolved findings and recorded visual sign-off by the art
reviewer, as well as relevant automated gates. It does not require inventing a
new universal contrast ratio or an unmeasured performance budget. The prototype
remains available for comparison after promotion.
