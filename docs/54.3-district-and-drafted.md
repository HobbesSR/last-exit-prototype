## District

### `block`

- **Role:** a large urban block that is several things at once: buildings,
  yards and alleys. It's the first type with a decomposer, and the one that
  makes the large set pieces read as places.
- **Strategy:** the SDK's decomposition (19, 20) splits the region into
  children and dispatches each to another type's builders: `hut`, `depot` or
  `compound` for rooms, `ruins` or `cover` for lobes, and `open` for the
  circulation left over. Region types may borrow each other's builders (19).
  **Built (#161, #580):** `map/micro/strategies/block.ts`. Its primary planner
  uses spacing instead of SDK decomposition. It splits its region by spacing,
  as `depot` does, so its primary path needs no route search and scales the same
  way. It reuses `hall`'s largest-rectangle finder and the other types'
  strategies. The bounded #580 pilot now uses SDK allocation only when that
  planner produces no lots. The fallback uses a deterministic work budget,
  selects at most one smaller rectangular lot and keeps a connected open
  circulation remainder; existing
  successful plans retain the original path (20.3.5). The budget is shared by
  attempted boxes, connectivity checks and SDK operations. Exhaustion is
  reported by `planBlock().fallbackWork` and raises an error in the physical
  builder. There is no former cell-count or axis-size guard; the budget limits
  work instead. This does not implement the broader
  multi-lot/decomposition or Building SDK milestone in #535.
  - **Builder mechanics:** [20.3.5](20.3.5-district-and-core-element-builders.md#block-builder).
  - **Core elements:** it sites none. Any its brief lists are left for the
    report.
  - **Too small:** a region with no viable 6 × 6 lot and its two usable
    frontages goes to `open` (17 M24).
- **Obligations:** every two children that share a boundary have at least
  one portal between them (51 stage 6, Corey 2026-10-01). Each child keeps its
  own promise, so the block's portals are joined by inference. As built, lots
  touch only the alleys, through their frontages, and a lot that would cut
  the alleys in two is refused (#508).
- **Shape needs:** *proposed*, at least two tiles' worth, 72 cells. As built,
  a contained 12 × 12 for one lot with its alley.
- **Formed by:** enormous set pieces (`city-block`, 52).
- **Parameters:** none yet. Each lot's type takes its own defaults.
- **Material:** `decomposition/example.ts` and the retired demonstration
  strategy `decomposition/realize.ts` (#483, in git history).

## Market

Market restores the old library's area identity (Corey, 2026-10-03 and
2026-10-04, 17.2.7). Its first dedicated production strategy is implemented
under #534; richer theme and geometry can follow the art direction.

### `market`

- **Role:** the old library's market. Rows of stalls and awnings with narrow
  lanes between them: dense, low cover that breaks sightlines everywhere and
  rewards close fights.
- **Strategy:** `buildMarket` composes counter stalls along the longer axis,
  alternating their facing across rows. Lanes are two cells wide, with a
  seeded wider cross-lane every four columns on large footprints. Optional
  awnings hide without blocking; no building enclosure is created.
  See [20.3.5](20.3.5-district-and-core-element-builders.md) for siting and limits.
- **Parameters:** `spacing` (integer at least 4, default 6), the pitch along
  a row; `roofed` (boolean, default true), individual stall awnings.
- **Shape needs:** a stall footprint (normally 4 by 2 cells) in the reduced
  fill and a complete two-cell bypass ring in the access mask. Smaller
  spacing permits two- or three-cell-long stalls. No fit falls back to open
  ground with zone loot; thin wings may stay empty.
- **Core elements:** none; requests remain for the report.
- **Formed by:** enormous and medium set pieces, where the old library's
  `market` pieces were.
