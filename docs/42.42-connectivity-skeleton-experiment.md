# 42.42. Connectivity skeleton: pre-fill spanning trees for sparse sides (#883)

Back to [42](42-performance-history.md). This experiment resolves the scaling blocker
identified in [42.40](42.40-sparse-sides-experiment.md) (#851): when tile sides are
sparse (`sparse / face`), placement at 36 × 18 failed across all seeds because large
sealed pockets of 60–72 slots formed long before the bounded reachability lookahead
([42.39](42.39-reachability-lookahead.md), 32 slots / 8 unplaced) could detect them.

Rather than running unbounded lookahead during the WFC solve, #883 guarantees global
connectivity upfront: before the fill search begins, placement draws a random spanning
tree of forced-open seams across the slot multigraph, ensuring every slot and set piece
connects into a single navigable backbone. All non-tree seams remain free.

The feature is off by default and exposed as an opt-in toggle and experiment in the Map
Lab (53.1) and `library-comparison.mts`. Bundled libraries, version hashes, and frozen
fixtures are unchanged.

## The mechanism

1. **Multigraph construction:**
   - Every fill slot is an independent node.
   - Each placed set piece (which may span multiple slots) is collapsed into a single node.
   - Outer set piece sides connect to adjacent slot nodes only through their authored passable openings.
     Between two nodes there may be multiple candidate seams (a multigraph).
   - Boundary seams outside the map or against impermeable margins are excluded.

2. **Spanning tree algorithms (`algorithm`):**
   - **Kruskal (balanced):** Randomized Kruskal with union-find and shuffled edge weights.
     Produces balanced branching trees with moderate path stretch.
   - **Wilson (uniform spanning tree):** Loop-erased random walk (Aldous-Broder / Wilson).
     Produces uniform random spanning trees with long, winding corridors.
   - **Prim (short spurs):** Randomized Prim growing from a random starting node.
     Produces dense networks with many short dead-end spurs.

3. **Extra loops and shortcuts (`extraOpenShare`, `shortcutPriority`):**
   - A pure spanning tree has no cycles. An opt-in fraction of non-tree seams (`extraOpenShare`, e.g. 15%)
     is opened to introduce loops.
   - When `shortcutPriority` is enabled, non-tree seams connecting nodes with large tree distances
     are prioritized for opening, effectively providing strategic macro bypasses.

4. **Multi-part slot domain filter:**
   - Tile designs may contain disjoint passable components separated by internal obstacles (e.g. rubble
     or hedge barriers). If a slot has multiple forced-open sides, any tile orientation whose internal parts
     cannot bridge all forced-open sides into a single connected component is pruned from that slot's WFC domain
     before solving starts.

5. **WFC `:must-open` constraints:**
   - Tree and extra open seams receive directional relations ending in `:must-open` (`E:must-open`, etc.).
   - Placement's adjacency compatibility enforces both geometric fit and mutual passability (`fits && connects`).
   - WFC ortho link discovery acknowledges `:must-open` relations, preventing spurious dead-end contradictions.

### Key observations

- **Eliminates sealed pocket stalls:** At 36 × 18 under `sparse / face`, all 3 seeds previously failed
  50 attempts due to sealed components of 60–72 slots. With the skeleton, all 3 seeds solve on
  **Attempt 0** without a single sealed-pocket refusal.
- **11× speedup at 24 wide, 2.3× speedup at 12 wide:** At width 24, generation drops from 52.0 s to 4.7 s.
  Because WFC domain filtering and forced seams channel the solve along guaranteed passable corridors,
  the solver spends virtually no time backtracking out of dead ends.
- **One-portal regions drastically reduced:** One-portal dead-end regions drop by more than half
  (110 → 40 at 12 wide; 408 → 147 at 24 wide) as the skeleton ensures slots connect across boundaries.

## Map Lab integration

The Map Lab Placement tab (53.1) includes:
- **Skeleton algorithm selector:** `#solveSkeleton` (`off`, `wilson`, `prim`, `kruskal`).
- **Extra open seam selector:** `#solveExtraOpen` (`0%`, `15%`, `30%`).
- **Seams overlay mode:** `Connectivity skeleton (#883)` under `#solveSeams`.
  - Spanning tree edges are rendered as bold teal lines (`#7adbc1`).
  - Extra open seams are rendered as dashed warm gold lines (`#f7cd8b`).
  - Dual graph center-to-center lines display the overall tree topology connecting slots.
