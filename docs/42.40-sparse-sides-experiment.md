# 42.40. Sparser tile sides: the experimental library (#851)

Back to [42](42-performance-history.md). The direction is 17.2.11's "Fewer portals per region" and
[52.1](52.1-perimeter-authoring.md): the half-and-half side (`hhhPPP`) stops being the default, for
fill tiles and set pieces alike, keeping whole 3-cell openings and letting placement keep regions
connected (51.3). Nothing here changes a bundled library, a version or the fixture. The libraries
are `sparseSidesLibrary` in `map/sparse-sides.ts`, which the Map Lab offers as a library source
(53.1); adoption is Corey's call, as one content batch (31, #878's sequencing comment on #851).

## The schemes

Only a side authored as `hhhPPP` (or `PPPhhh`) changes, and it changes whole: as authored, or
fully hinted. So every opening that remains is a whole 3-cell block.

- **Fill** (`fill`): `bundled`, or `sparse`. Under `sparse`, each fillable tile with two or more
  half-and-half sides (`cover-ground`, `park-ground`, `hut-quarter`) becomes a family whose
  members keep one or two of those sides open and hint the rest. The all-open tile keeps 10% of
  the family's weight as the fallback (52.1), and the old `-closed-*` variants give way to the
  family.
- **Set pieces** (`setPieces`): every scheme but `bundled` hints the seams between a set piece's
  own slots, so no internal guaranteed run forms inside it. Then the outer sides:
  - `inner`: as authored;
  - `alternate`: walking each outer face, every other slot side stays open, from the first;
  - `face`: one opening per outer face, at its middle slot.

  Slots are rewritten per set piece (`<tile>-sp-<sides>`, `fillable: false`). Slots whose
  orientation is chosen at placement, such as rotatable rubble banks, keep their tiles.

**Set pieces side by side (#856).** A closed set-piece side can't meet another piece's open one,
so with closed borders nearly every attempt failed before its first choice: on `size-12-1`, every
one of the 50. The sparse libraries state the experimental `fitPlacedSetPieces: true`, and
placement then offers only anchors, members and orientations that fit pieces already placed
(51.3). Bundled libraries don't state it, so their maps don't move.

- **Set-piece borders are the lever.** `alternate` halves the portals on set-piece regions at
  every size, and `face` cuts them about sevenfold.
- **The sparse fill moves little.** The fill's `hhhPPP` tiles are a small share of it. Most fill
  portals come from `any` sides (`field`, the corner, edge and through families): against another
  region's `any`, a whole 6-cell side opens. Closing those is a further authoring step, outside
  #851's half-and-half scope.
- **`inner` alone changes portals little,** since seams inside one set piece aren't portals; it
  removes internal strips (730 → 349 at 12 wide).
- **`face` makes placement slow, and with the sparse fill it fails at 36 wide.** Each 36 × 18
  seed was last refused for a sealed market component of 2,160 to 2,592 cells (60–72 slots), twice
  the lookahead's bound (32 slots, 8 unplaced; [42.39](42.39-reachability-lookahead.md)). Such
  pockets are certain long before they close, but the bounded lookahead can't see them. #883
  proposes building connectivity in before the fill (a spanning tree of seams that must open);
  unbounded early detection is the alternative it compares.

- **Routes lengthen, a little.** At 36 wide the contestant median goes from 1.030 to 1.041–1.045
  and the p90 from 1.076 to 1.094–1.122; hunters' p90 reaches 1.216 at 24 wide. Two maps had one
  hunter route in 24 (4.2%) above 1.5 (`bundled / alternate`'s `size-24-3`, `sparse / alternate`'s
  `size-12-2`), where the bundled library had none.
- **Dead-end area grows** at 24 and 36 wide, most with the sparse fill (36 wide: 0.82% to 1.16% for
  contestants, 1.04% to 1.64% for hunters).
- **Closing guarantees mostly closes boundary.** The unguaranteed share rises by 6–8 points, and
  79–82% of it is blocked, against 72–76%.
- **Generation is no slower** in these single, measuring runs: fewer portals mean less to clear
  and connect. They are indicative; `tools/generation-time.mjs` under the bench lock is the
  measure for an adoption batch.
- As in 42.37, long routes still find the remaining guaranteed openings, so straightness moves
  less than the portal counts. #883's skeleton is the experiment that decides where those
  openings are.
