# 42.40. Regional theatrical lighting experiment

Issue #771; adoption batch #878. Contract: 29.4 and 51.9. Recipe:
`map/micro/lighting.ts`, `district-light-1`. Adopted by default in `types-67` (#878).

The emitter divides a composed map into at most six broad districts. Regional
land-use cell counts choose the treatment; an independent named stream over
canonical region IDs/seeds breaks ties and varies intensity. Origin translation
is shared with geometry. This uses the existing `map.appearance.lighting.fields`
contract; no macro or saved-region schema change is involved.

The narrow evidence is deliberately synthetic rather than a whole-map seed sweep:
`tests/micro-lighting.test.js` covers independent RNG/ID order, unchanged stamped
geometry, reorder/translation invariance, field bounds, normalized overlapping
samples, long-surface splitting and array-identity cache behavior. The art witness
builds six small actual open/park/rubble regions through the normal builders and
composition adapter. Its WebGL samples compare lit/unlit states, then toggle
lighting to invalidate tiles and require the rebuilt lit pixels to match exactly.
`test-results/art-witness/procedural-lighting.json` retains fields and samples;
`procedural-regional-lighting.png` is the contact-sheet capture.

The initial passing pixel probe recorded lit colors 3754335, 7831916, 5592394;
unlit 3884365, 7831916, 3884365. Rebuilt values matched lit values exactly.
The park-center probe at (600, 200) does not change: the `park-front-6` hedge
(x=600, y=120, 40 by 280) covers the lit ground wash. Its existing `ink.hedge`
draw branch does not consume the resolved field tint; this is not an overhead
tree-canopy effect. The assertion requires a visible change somewhere, not that
every obstacle is tinted. Boundary blends and local surface splitting are checked at the resolver
level rather than inferred from those three pixels.

Verification: the fast suite passed 1,479 tests (135.9 s); the five focused lighting
tests passed and measured 0.2 s isolated in the fast tier (15 s budget). These
small fixtures add no full-chain generation. The art witness passed 41 captures;
the new case adds six tiny region builds and three cache/toggle settles to the
existing browser gate, rather than another browser launch or production map build.
Type checking and static checks also passed. Timings describe this local run,
not an assumed CI runtime.

The full default chain sweep (`node map/tools/cli.mts sweep --check
map/tools/fixtures/chain-baseline.json`) checked 228 entries in 485 s with no drift
and exit 0. No baseline was regenerated.

Limits: the six-field budget bounds existing client sampling cost; this is not a
claim of zero additional render or serialization cost. Emission scans owned cells
once for bounds and once for land-use votes; it adds no simulation tick work.
Rectangles can span unowned holes. The grid is a first district composition, not
a learned district/landmark layout, and fixture evidence does not establish art
quality over the production seed population. Corey authorized adoption in #878, with subjective visual evaluation and feedback
surfaced incidentally during general testing rather than a separate review gate.

PR #879 review 693 adds a four-map production probe (seeds 1, 42, 771, 2026),
run by the reviewer: six fields each; 20 of 24 districts cobalt, three cyan, one
amber and none magenta. Unmapped types vote cobalt (29.4), and numerous open/cover
regions make that bucket dominant. Amber focal edges appeared in 22 of 24
districts, so the current core/landmark rule does not establish selective focal
composition. These are adoption limitations tracked in #878, not a requirement
to change the off-by-default recipe in this PR. The reviewer also noted that
existing hedge, pipe, window and ruin-wall draw branches retain their own ink
colors over lit ground; expanding field coverage makes that pre-existing behavior
more visible.
