# 56.2. Ground Decals at Doors and Windows

Active map compatibility domains are owned by [53](53-map-artifacts-and-tools.md#current-compatibility-domains). Version values in dated change records below describe those checkpoints.

Status: **implemented**, 2026-10-14 (#293). Design note on the ground-plane decal primitive
that marks where a roofed building can be entered or seen into. Read with
[56.1](56.1-roofs.md) for the roofs that cover those openings, and [29](29-geometry-and-drawing.md)
for the drawing planes.

## 1. Problem and Direction

A roof covers its building's doors and windows until it fades, so from outside an entrance is
hard to find. Corey's direction (#233): entrances carry a variety of thematic markings on the
ground that extend out a little, "a quarter to a third" of a cell, "whatever looks good". His
follow-up on #293: windows get decals too, and every decal must extend beyond what the roof
occludes, which already overhangs the structure.

A decal is presentation. It never collides, never blocks sight or navigation, and is not
discovered or hidden: it is static map geometry like a floor.

## 2. Data Shapes

```ts
/** A ground-plane marking: presentation only, never collision, sight or navigation (56.2). */
export interface Decal extends Box {
  /** Chosen by the builder; the renderer styles by it and falls back to `plain`. */
  theme: string;
  /** What it marks. */
  marks: 'door' | 'window';
  /** The direction it extends from its wall, away from the building. */
  side: 'N' | 'E' | 'S' | 'W';
}
```

`ElementTemplate.decals?: Decal[]` holds them in template-local coordinates, and `placeElement`
stamps them onto `GameMap.decals?: Decal[]` in world coordinates, in template order.

Decals are a template field, like roofs, not an `ElementPart`. Parts are emitted in a frozen
order, draw ids from the shared serial, and are indexed by `realizeBuilding`'s `owners` and
counted by every strategy's manifest. A decal has no id and no body, so it stays out of all of
that, and stamping one never renumbers what is placed after it.

## 3. Derivation

`openingDecals` (`map/micro/building/decals.ts`) derives them from a realization's placed
openings. Every `door` and `window` with `OUTSIDE` on one side gets one; an `open` side and an
interior opening do not.
- **Across** the wall it spans the opening exactly.
- **Out** from the footprint's edge it reaches `ROOF_OVERHANG + DECAL_REACH × cellSize`, where
  `DECAL_REACH` is a quarter. The roof hides the first `ROOF_OVERHANG` (12) while it is drawn, so
  a quarter cell always shows beyond it: 10 of 22 units on a 40 cell, 12 of 24 on a 48.
- Under the wall it starts at the footprint edge. The part under the roof shows when the roof
  fades.

`realizeBuilding` emits them on its template, themed by its `decalTheme` option (default
`plain`; `false` emits none). `splitBuilding` gives each piece the decals of the openings whose
non-`OUTSIDE` side is its space, so a compound room keeps its own door's marking.

Strategies choose the theme by region type: `hut`, `depot` and `compound`.

## 4. Drawing

The renderer draws decals on the ground plane, after floors and before obstacles, into the
static tile cache. Per-theme styling stays in the scene ([29](29-geometry-and-drawing.md)):
- `plain`: a worn pale strip.
- `hut`: a dirt path that widens away from the door.
- `depot`: yellow and black hazard stripes across a door; a pale sill under a window.
- `compound`: a stone step with a darker lip.

A window's decal is fainter than a door's in every theme: light falling from it, not a step.

## 5. Versioning

- **Wire version 8:** unchanged. `decals` is an optional `GameMap` field, as `roofs` is; maps
  and the frozen fixture without it draw no decals.
- **Region types:** `types-28`, since every building strategy's output gains decals. The sweep's
  results and built views move; nothing walkable changes, so the report does not ([53](53-map-artifacts-and-tools.md)).
