## Candidate and interpretation

One four-cell strip crosses the playable mask north to south at the map's
midpoint. Two experimental owners meet on a one-cell-thick physical wall with
one four-cell opening a quarter of the way down. Their only shared guaranteed
portal is the central two cells of that opening: its automatic endpoint caps
preserve all four physical cells. Each builder supplies its own half-cell wall,
within its fill and ownership. Macro still prescribes classes and crossing
promises, not compulsory geometry. No hint is promoted to an impassable contract.

This is one fixed candidate, not a search for good route statistics. The supported
strip is rectangular and core-class free; unsupported inputs refuse without
trying another position. Experimental per-slot tile designs and matching
set-piece/tile-set definitions derive the resolved classes, region boundaries,
proof and briefs through the ordinary stages. Newly shortened one-cell portal
remnants are trimmed once, with every segment recorded. No iterative repair or
geometry tuning follows measurement.

The mask and resolved classes outside the strip stay exact. Inside it, ownership
changes; region splitting, seeds, automatic margins and changed-builder output
are disclosed confounders. Unchanged briefs retain exact historical results;
changed ordinary briefs use current builders and existing loot filtering.
Complete experimental saves embed those decisions and rederive their briefs.
Their separate build identity names current and historical input content; the
production reader and ordinary layout-only regeneration refuse them. This
neither promotes a new production type nor changes the compatibility domains in
[53](53-map-artifacts-and-tools.md).

Fixed world-space before endpoints remain the basis for movement comparisons.
Every failure, invalid endpoint and removed old loot target stays explicit;
actual final targets are checked separately. Changed-owner SDK proofs supplement
the macro graph/core checks. They do not replace measured physical boundary
sweeps or imply that all arbitrary interior sites are reachable.

## Disposition and epic boundary

**Retain** the opt-in, source-bound boundary/crossing witness. The existing
declarative model can represent a physically controlled shared boundary without
inventing a mandatory impassable prescription or lying in saved briefs.
Target-relative and some map-scale detour respond to the physical partition.

**Adapt selectively** if a future selected authorship task needs such a
partition: it needs purposeful topology/crossings and an explicit content scope,
not promotion of this abstract curtain. Broad progression is still limited,
loot/owner regeneration is substantial, disconnected mesh pieces remain, and
construction/proof costs are material. This comparison does not choose boundary
control as the epic's main lever or prove that fewer declared portals alone help.

**Defer** production promotion, blanket boundary requirements, additional gate
search, general #649 growth, density tuning, all-builder rewrites and park
polish. The task stops after this one candidate. #529 still owns much-less-empty
space and broader exploration/backtracking; #535 owns broader decomposition,
#531 remaining composition, #649 optional growth, #707 contrasting-family
evaluation and #581/#586 authorship. Reassess those functional gaps under
[32](32-delegation.md); completing more curtain variants or rare-case polish is
not an automatic successor. A new useful authored topology capability has more
potential reach than refining this lab comparison, but requires selection and
requirements review at pickup.
