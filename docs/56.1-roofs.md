# 56.1. Declared Roofs and Geometry

Active map compatibility domains are owned by [53](53-map-artifacts-and-tools.md#current-compatibility-domains). Version values in dated change records below describe those checkpoints.

Status: **implemented**, 2026-10-13 (#292). Design note on declared contiguous roofs
on the cell/segment grid, their relationship to `Building`, sight rules, and versioning.
Read with [56](56-buildings.md) for the building SDK, [29](29-geometry-and-drawing.md)
for geometry and overhead drawing, and [15](15-information-rules.md) for sight rules.

## 1. Problem and Direction

Historically, roof coverage was defined solely by rectangular `Building` bounding boxes.
This imposed severe geometric constraints:
- Realization refused `encloses: true` on concave or holed footprints (`realize.ts`).
- `splitBuilding` required every roofed space to be a single filled rectangle.
- Multi-room assemblies (such as compound rings) lacked structural contiguity linkages,
  relying on fragile client-side door-edge adjacency graphs to infer grouping.
- Points inside the outdoor notches of concave buildings were misclassified as indoors
  by rectangular bounding box checks.

Under #292, builders declare contiguous roofs on the cell/segment grid, matching how
walls and rooms are constructed.

## 2. Data Shapes

The schema introduces cell-grid roof primitives in `shared/types.ts` and `shared/map/element.ts`:

```ts
export interface Cell {
  x: number;
  y: number;
}

export interface Roof {
  /** Unique on the map: its building's id, else a placement-scoped name (below). */
  id: string;
  /** Presentation group identifier for approach and interior fading. */
  groupId: string;
  /** Axis-aligned bounding box in world coordinates. */
  bounds: Box;
  /** World anchor where cell (0, 0) is located. */
  origin: Vec2;
  /** Grid cell size in world units (e.g. 40 or 48). */
  cellSize: number;
  /** Component cells relative to `origin`. */
  cells: Cell[];
  /** Exterior perimeter overhang in world units (default 12). */
  overhang?: number | undefined;
  /** Exterior doorway approach cells that reveal the roof (29). */
  thresholds?: Cell[] | undefined;
  /** Optional back-reference to the enclosing semantic Building. */
  buildingId?: BuildingId | undefined;
}
```

On `ElementTemplate` (`shared/map/element.ts`), `ElementRoof` carries local template cells,
local bounds, `cellSize`, a template-local `id`, and optionally a `groupId` with a `groupScope`.
`placeElement` gives each roof world coordinates and names it within its **placement scope**,
the placement's building id, or `roofs-N` (N counting the map's roofs before it) when the
element encloses no building. A roof's id is its building's id when it is that building's only
roof, and `<scope>.<template id>` otherwise. So a reused template, even a concave one in one
region, never repeats an id. Neither name draws from the shared id serial, so stamping roofs
never renumbers the obstacles, doors and items placed after them.

A `groupId` is template-local, and stamping qualifies it:
- `groupScope: 'element'` (the default) is `<placement scope>/<groupId>`. Each placement of a
  template is its own group, so two houses from one template never fade together.
- `groupScope: 'region'` is `<nodeId>/<groupId>`, shared by every element its region stamps.
  It is for one assembly placed as several elements: `splitBuilding({ groupId })` marks its
  pieces this way, and the builder keeps the id unique in its region.
- No `groupId` means the roof is a group of one.

`realizeBuilding` and `splitBuilding` take `groupId` as one group for every roof, or as a
space → group record (`BuildingRoofGroups`). A space the record leaves out fades alone. In
`realizeBuilding` a roof is a contiguous run of roofed cells and may span spaces; it takes their
one group, and a roof spanning spaces in different groups is an issue, not a guess.

The builder states the policy; the helper applies it deterministically. The one policy is
**reachability** (#348, Corey's decision): spaces fade together when one can walk between
them without leaving the structure. `reachableRoofGroups(realization, allocation, prefix)`
unions the spaces joined by a placed door or open side, including through unroofed
spaces such as a passage. A window joins nothing, and neither does an opening to `OUTSIDE`.
Room labels and the door-versus-open distinction don't matter. A group is named
`<prefix>-<first space in allocation order>`, and a space nothing joins is left out.

The compound uses it with its brief id as the prefix. Its rooms open onto the court, so only a
corner room and the neighbour it opens into fade together. The whole-ring group (#343) faded
the ring as one across its unroofed gate passages and is retired.

## 3. Relationship to `Building`

`Building` (`map.buildings`) and `Roof` (`map.roofs`) serve distinct, complementary roles:

1. **`Building` is semantic and administrative:**
   - Holds `BuildingId` and `nodeId`.
   - Tags loot spots and doors (`buildingId`).
   - Retains rectangular footprints for legacy catalogue templates and stored arenas.
2. **`Roof` is geometric and perceptual:**
   - Defines exact cell-by-cell coverage, including concave L-shapes, U-shapes, and courtyards.
   - Computes exterior overhang and doorway approach thresholds.
   - Drives overhead rendering, camera zoom, and sight occlusion.

When an element specifies declared roofs, `roofAt(map, point)` resolves containment via
`pointInRoof(roof, point)`, ensuring outdoor notches and holes remain truly outdoors.
Undeclared legacy buildings continue to resolve through their rectangular bounding boxes,
ensuring mixed maps and legacy maps work seamlessly without data conversion.

## 4. Invariant Sight Rules

Per [15](15-information-rules.md) and [29](29-geometry-and-drawing.md), **sight rules stay as is**:
- A viewer outside a roof cannot see someone under it.
- Someone under a roof cannot see someone under another roof, but can see outside.
- Two entities under the **exact same roof** (`eyeRoof.id === targetRoof.id`) see each other.

Crucially, **presentation grouping (`groupId`) does not alter sight**:
- `groupId` coordinates cosmetic alpha fading across the rooms its builder groups (`roofGroups`).
- Rooms sharing a `groupId` still conceal each other unless they are the same room (`id`).
- No cross-room authoritative sight leaks exist.

Drawing drops a roof's overhang (12 px), dark perimeter insets (4 px), and boundary seam strokes (4 px)
where it physically abuts another roof or building on the map (`roofCellBorders`), regardless of
presentation `groupId` (#349). Adjacency compares boundary contact intervals with positive overlap length,
supporting unequal cell sizes (e.g. 40 and 48) and staggered origins. Different fade groups fade
independently without seam lines, dark gaps, or double overhang.

`roofAt` reads an index cached per map object (`getRoofIndex`). A map is treated as immutable
after generation: the client replaces the object on every map change (`changeMap`).

## 5. Schema and Wire Versioning

- **Wire Version 8:** Unchanged. `roofs?: Roof[]` is an optional field on `GameMap`.
  Legacy maps and frozen test fixtures (such as `behavior-nav-1.json.gz`) omit `roofs`,
  falling back completely to `buildings`.
- **Region Types Version:** Bumped from `types-25` to `types-26` in `map/micro/region-types.ts`,
  recording the generation batch output change as strategies emit element templates with
  declared roofs. #348 bumped it to `types-27`, since the compound's roofs change group. #293's ground decals
  bumped it to `types-28` ([56.2](56.2-ground-decals.md)).
