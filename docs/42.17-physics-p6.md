## Limits

Finite-force ropes have soft distance targets: contacts win over length, and
there is no hard positional correction or accumulated launch impulse. Hook tips
use a zero-radius sweep, combat actor disks and present authoritative motion;
solid props block without becoming targets. There is no wrapping, scenery grab,
historical rewind, client-authoritative hit, damage or new default activation.
Full P8/P9 navigation, human feel and production capacity qualification remain.

The full browser command encounters the existing overhead-roof seam pixel
assertion at `tests/overhead.browser.mjs:143`. It failed twice with this branch
and also with `forgejo/main`'s original renderer substituted at the browser route;
that baseline failure is separate from hook rendering. No pixel expectation was
relaxed. Browser logs are retained locally under `test-results/p6-*`.
