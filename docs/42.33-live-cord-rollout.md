# 42.33. Live-cord rollout: batched commits, the last five builders, cheaper searches

Back to [42](42-performance-history.md). Issue #787, following [42.31](42.31-rubble-plant-live-cord.md)
and [42.32](42.32-cord-generation-cost.md). Content version `types-63`. The coordinator is in
[20.10](20.10-portal-cords.md); builder mechanics are in 20.3.3–20.3.5 and 54.1–54.3.

## Change

Three things, in one adoption PR (31, "Content changes: one bump per batch").

1. **Batched commits.** Builders site a phase on a detached draft (`stage()`) and prove the cord
   once for as many pieces as stand together (`commitEach`; `commitWhile` for pieces of one element).
   Rubble and plant moved first. A refused batch splits in half, down to single pieces.
2. **The last five builders on a live cord.** Cover, ruins, depot, hall and market no longer read
   frozen `protectedRoutes`. They keep only the portal approaches and place through `liveCord`
   (`strategies/scatter.ts`).
   - A cord-refused piece is passed over: a ruins span is left out as broken wall, and a hall
     keeps pillars in order, then walls, while a cord stands (greedy, favouring earlier bays).
   - Cover's post-filters need no cord of their own. `coverAround` only removes pieces, which
     can't break a cord. `coverNear` adds its guard through a live cord.
   - `protectedRoutes` left production. It survived as `frozenRoutes`
     (`map/micro/experiments/frozen-routes.ts`, removed in #923) only for #663's fixed-route depot control and the
     tools that measure against frozen routes.
3. **Two exact search savings** in `discoverCordWitness`:
   - Sweeps test only the blockers a coarse grid files near their capsule.
   - One mesh search finds every later stand's corridor at once.

   Both leave every output byte-identical (below).

Envelope claims (`probeClaim`) were the plan and were set aside; 20.10 says why. Corey's framing,
2026-11-01, mid-issue: there is one contract, the portal promise. The SDK offers mechanisms,
builders meet the promise as they see fit, and the live cord should be cheap enough to suffice
for the current builders.

## Method

As 42.31.

- **Panel:** `size-{12,24,36}-1` with #688's parameters.
- **Content and navigation:** `node tools/park-route-audit.mjs --type T`. "Before" rebuilds the
  type's briefs with main's strategy (`5e30b4b`). Its `protectedRoutes` import resolves to the same
  function in `frozen-routes.ts` (removed in #923).
- **Retained reports:** `summary.json` and `examples.json.gz` are in
  [reports/issue-787-cover](reports/issue-787-cover/),
  [-ruins](reports/issue-787-ruins/), [-depot](reports/issue-787-depot/),
  [-hall](reports/issue-787-hall/) and [-market](reports/issue-787-market/).
- **Whole-map time:** `node tools/generation-time.mjs`, three sequential runs per map under the
  bench lock, with the machine otherwise quiet.
- **Per-builder search counts:** a temporary wrapper around `discoverCordWitness`, not committed.

Proofs passed on every changed region of the panel: `validateFillConstraints`, a thorough
`validatePortalReach` and a rebuild with reversed cell and zone order. Every map passed `checkMap`
with diagnosis, every core element was found, and every final spawn → exit and spawn → charger
target completed for both bodies.

## Cost

Cord searches at size-36-1, counted in the same build. Main is `5e30b4b` (types-62); "rollout"
is this change before the two search savings.

| Builder | Searches, main | Searches, rollout |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Rubble | 2,873 (211 failed) | 2,371 (380 failed) |
| Plant, with infill | 153 | 18 |
| Ruins | — | 19 |
| Depot | — | 35 |
| Market | — | 19 |
| Hall | — | 6 |
| Cover | — | 267 |
| `liveCord` setups (all types) | 198 | 860 |

Batching left few searches, but each one in a large, many-portal region was dear. One ruins
region of 60 portals and 8,064 cells took 8.7 s to build, and one depot of 54 portals took 5.3 s.

A profile of the 28 market, depot and ruins regions at 36 wide (25 s in all) found:
- 11 s in sweeps, each testing every blocker in the region;
- 8.5 s in `freePaths`, a full mesh search per portal stand;
- 2.2 s building meshes.

The two exact savings target those:

- **Indexed sweeps.** `discoverCordWitness` files the blockers by a grid four cells across, and a
  sweep tests only those filed under its capsule's box. A blocker whose box misses the capsule's
  cannot overlap it, so `travelClear` answers the same.
- **One corridor search.** At the first stand that needs the mesh, one `freePaths` call finds
  every later stand's corridor. Its heap pops by key and then index, whatever else is queued, and
  stands relax nothing, so each corridor is the one a search for that stand alone finds.

The same 28 regions then build in 7.1 s, and the worst ruins in 1.7 s, with a byte-identical
hash of their results. Whole-map digests match too.

| Map | main (types-62) | Rollout, no savings | types-63 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| size-12-1 | 5.07 s | 4.29 s | 3.56 s |
| size-24-1 | 21.81 s | 22.49 s | 18.58 s |
| size-36-1 | 46.69 s | 65.66 s | 46.33 s |

Runs agree within 0.5 s, apart from one 48.8 s main run at 36 wide. The rollout's digests are
identical with and without the savings: `7a7a3325297e3ec8`, `dec0855c3cd0a0d4` and
`0cc4afcc06a3985b`. So every builder now places on a live cord at no more generation cost than
main. 36 wide is still 15 s above `types-61`'s 30.8 s (42.31).

Per-type timings in the audit reports were taken before the savings and overstate the live cord.

## Witness limits (#801, 2026-11-04)

The macro-sized depot test (200 × 150, 170 portals) went from about 2 s to 459 s alone with this
rollout, and to 20 minutes in CI, which timed out every pull request's `verify` (#790). All of
the cost was the cord, and none of it geometry: every failed search reported `budget`. The
witness repeats each shared prefix once per stand, so its points grow with area times portals,
and two limits sat at `MAX_INPUTS = 16,384`: the witness's path points and the blockers'
outline points. Past either, every later commit failed, and `commitEach` split each failure into
log n more whole-region proofs while refusing pieces that fit.

| Depot (seed 3, a portal every 4 cells) | Peak witness points | Before | `MAX_POINTS = 2^18` |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 100 × 75, 84 portals | 12,500 | 1.2 s, 4 meshes | the same |
| 125 × 94, 104 portals | — | 22 s, 98 meshes, 97 rows | 1.6 s, 4 meshes, 101 rows |
| 150 × 112, 126 portals | — | 110 s, 390 meshes, 107 rows | 2.5 s, 4 meshes, 150 rows |
| 200 × 150, 170 portals | 35,000 (blockers 16,600) | 459 s | 5.7 s, 4 meshes, 724 elements |

The full chain sweep (`sweep --check`, 224 maps) shows no drift: no game map reached the old
limits, so this is not a content change. The fast tier bounds the 125 × 94 case's searches and
meshes, which the old limit would fail; `tests/slow/micro-depot-scale.test.js` bounds the macro
case's (31).

## What is left

- **Exact:** `discoverCordWitness` still re-checks region cells and the access mask on each call
  (42.32).
- **Not exact:** an incremental mesh, or local re-proof of only the touched segments. Each would
  change which cords are found.
- **Frozen routes remain only as #663's fixed control and in its tools (retired in #923).**
