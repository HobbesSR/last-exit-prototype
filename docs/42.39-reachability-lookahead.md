# 42.39. Placement's reachability lookahead

Back to [42](42-performance-history.md). Issue #857, following [42.37](42.37-matched-adoption.md)'s
heavy tail: on the matched libraries some placement attempts churned among their last few slots
until the stall limit (`PLACEMENT_STALL_LIMIT`, 500 refusals without a new depth) gave them up.
The rule now refuses a sealed pocket as soon as it is certain, and the stall limit didn't fire on
any measured seed. Validity, the matching rules and the content are unchanged. Layouts move,
because the search refuses some branches sooner, so the chain sweep is recaptured (below).

## What the stalls were

The open-face rule ([51.3](51.3-placement.md)) floods only settled cells, and stops at the first
face an unplaced slot could still join. A pocket is caught only once its last open face closes. By
then the choices that walled it in may lie far back. The solver backjumps to the latest slot on its
cut, tries alternatives that seal it again, and churns. Logging every refusal of the attempts that
hit the limit showed one area refused hundreds of times at growing sizes:

| Seed | Refused over and over |
| --- | --- |
| 12 × 6 `sweep-66` | a cover component at 72 to 540 cells, 616 times |
| 36 × 18 `seed-7` | a park component at 324 to 576 cells |
| Playground `wfc-large-1` | a rubble component (36 to 432 cells) in one attempt, a cover one (108 to 324) in the next |

The issue's cheapest variant, flooding only through unplaced slots and stopping at any face that
joins a settled cell, never fired on `sweep-1` to `-30`: refusals stayed at 1,052. The pockets
aren't holes alone. They are settled areas whose only open faces lead into a hole or two, and
nothing those holes can hold joins the rest of the map.

## The change

- **Lookahead.** After the open-face rule's pass, a second flood runs on through unplaced slots and
  unsettled cells. Each unplaced slot is one node that joins whatever its remaining options could
  join, and two unplaced slots always join. An area that can't reach the rest of the map is refused
  while it is still a hole, naming the slots that closed its sides, as a sealed component is named
  by its cut. Unplaced slots are judged optimistically, so a refusal is always sound.
- **Bounds.** The flood takes an area as open once it exceeds `LOOKAHEAD_CELLS` (32 slots of cells)
  or reaches more than `LOOKAHEAD_SLOTS` (8) unplaced slots. These bound each check's cost, not
  validity: a larger sealed area is still refused by the open-face rule when it closes.
- **Cost.** Both floods now take each placed design's fixed parts (cells joined inside it by class
  or a guaranteed segment, which the design alone decides) as one node, leaving it by its exits.
  Floods are breadth first, so an open flood marks the cells around the placed slots before running
  far. Segment prescriptions are read from a numbered table per design, not by string key. With the
  lookahead off, the 30 seeds `sweep-1` to `-30` make the same refusals and search nodes as before,
  seed for seed, and 36 × 18 seeds took 4.4 s against `main`'s 5.2 s.

## Choosing the bounds

Search nodes are deterministic, and the machine was shared, so the bounds were compared on the
stalled seeds by refusals, worst stall and flood nodes (per solve, `seed-1` as an unstalled control):

| Cells | Unplaced slots | `sweep-66` | `seed-7` | `wfc-large-1` | Flood nodes, `seed-1` |
| ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 288 | any | 665 refusals, limit fired | 786, limit fired | 1,858, limit fired | 77,537 |
| 576 | any | 20, worst 13 | 952, limit fired | 430, worst 50 | — |
| 1,152 | any | 20, worst 13 | 168, worst 49 | 430, worst 50 | 202,769 |
| 1,152 | 8 | 20, worst 13 | 168, worst 49 | 430, worst 50 | 164,618 |
| 1,152 | 4 | 73, worst 54 | 396, worst 221 | 430, worst 50 | 130,837 |

The pockets refused with no size bound on `sweep-1` to `-30` were mostly 4 slots (median 144 cells,
largest 612), with 1 unplaced slot in 7 of 9 and at most 6.

## Results

`node map/tools/placement-probe.mts` on the issue's seeds. Times are `main` (`9fa13f7`) against this
branch, each seed's pair run back to back in alternating order under the `bench` lock. Refusals,
stalls and attempts compare the branch with the lookahead off (`--lookahead 0`), which searches as
`main` does.

| Seeds | Stall limit fired | Worst stall | Refusals | Attempts | `main` | Branch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 12 × 6 `sweep-1` to `-120` | 1 → 0 | 501 → 116 | 4,932 → 2,179 | 385 → 383 | 82.1 s, worst 2.3 s | 88.9 s, worst 1.7 s |
| 36 × 18 `size-36-5` | 1 → 0 | 501 → 9 | 771 → 99 | 2 → 1 | 21.5 s | 5.6 s |
| 36 × 18 `seed-1` to `-10` | 2 → 0 | 501 → 90 | 3,826 → 1,418 | 12 → 10 | 71.3 s, worst 14.2 s | 55.5 s, worst 5.7 s |
| Playground `wfc-large-1` | 1 → 0 | 501 → 50 | 2,417 → 430 | 3 → 1 | 28.4 s | 7.3 s |

No seed failed. Of the 263 attempts that failed on the branch across these 132 seeds, 222 were
set pieces that didn't fit (#856); the other 41 failed in the solve, none at the stall limit.

**What it costs.** Where the lookahead changes nothing, it is overhead: the 103 of 120 seeds at
12 × 6 whose refusals are unchanged took 76.1 s against 66.2 s (+15%), and the five such 36 × 18
seeds (`seed-1`, `-4`, `-5`, `-8`, `-9`) 27.8 s against 24.5 s (+13%). Where it changes the search
it saves more: the 17 other 12 × 6 seeds took 12.8 s against 15.9 s, and every stalled seed sped
up three- to fourfold. Placement is a small part of generating a whole map (42.38).

## Layouts

The sweep was recaptured last (`sweep --out`, 458 s) with no defects. 61 of its 228 maps moved:
17 of the 120 game maps (the 17 seeds whose search changed above), 4 crowded, 4 sparse, and 36
playgrounds (8 at 12 × 6, 13 at 4 × 2, 12 at 6 × 3, the 3 quick 2 × 1). The larger sizes' briefs
(`tests/fixtures/large-briefs.json.gz`) and live map seed 2's pinned ids moved too, and were
recaptured; the room arena (seed 9, legacy content) did not move. `PLACEMENT_STALL_LIMIT` stays as
the backstop it was meant to be.
