## Boundary cost

The current tree times only `tick_begin` and `tick_finish` as core. Subtracting their medians from their enclosing group medians gives 119-125 us without traps and 135-166 us with traps. Relative to tick plus snapshot that is 2.3-2.5% and 2.5-2.6%, above the former 1% exception. Relative to the core calls' combined 630-643 us and 657-761 us it is 18.5-19.7% and 20.6-21.8%, above the requested 10% comparison. This is a grouped wrapper-minus-core estimate, not a direct measurement of crossing alone: it includes all work in the wrappers and profiler timing overhead. The original 42.58 budget used the TypeScript module cost as denominator; the core-time comparison is the requested additional proxy, not proof of that original ratio.

The separately timed pack is 55-56 us without traps (1.1% of the tick) and 61-66 us with traps (1.0-1.2%); apply, including copying and interpreting the output, is 58-65 us (1.1-1.3%) and 78-100 us (1.5-1.6%). Core is 630-643 us (12.3-12.6%) and 657-761 us (12.2-12.3%). Apply ranks above pack in these runs, but the timers do not isolate individual candidates: per-tick hazard and phase-six static blocks, phase-one player upload, and marker keys cannot be ranked by measured share. No fix is made here.

Some cost is transitional: #1215 specifies moving bots, navigation and input consumption into the core and one tick crossing; #1276 plans a single change list. Neither issue establishes how much mirror work will disappear, so the eventual saving is not measured here. Static blocks can be considered in #1276.

## Method and limits

Windows, Node 24.15.0; current `0eb41ff1` with the profiler labels in this change, versus `92df3a9d`, each with its own release WASM. `tests/bench-phase-three.mjs` used the same cached 24x12 map, seed 4217, 300 warm and 600 timed ticks, five rounds per run, three alternating pairs each with and without `--traps`. All 12 runs were below the 25% external-busy threshold (4.99-8.63%). Each run reports process CPU and wall in its contention stamp; whole-run CPU includes setup and cannot be attributed to the tick. Timers were added to the scratch baseline around the two otherwise unlabelled groups; current pack/core/apply timers add instrumentation overhead to the comparison. Each reported group is the median of per-round means. This workload is one map and bot trace, and does not independently exercise or isolate every rare event or historical exception-used module. It cannot establish individual module compliance with 42.58's original TypeScript-denominator rule.
