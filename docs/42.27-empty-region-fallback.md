# 42.27. Empty-region fallback

Back to [42](42-performance-history.md). Issue #717 under #529; direction in
[17.2.11](17.2.11-navigation-structure-direction.md#independent-region-edges-and-useful-fallback).
Builder mechanics are in [20.3.3](20.3.3-ground-and-friction-builders.md) (cover) and
[20.3.4](20.3.4-structure-builders.md#park-builder) (park); content version `types-59`.

## What was empty

Panel: `size-{12,24,36}-{1,2,3}`, the nine maps #716 used, from `generate(seed, params, undefined, 48)`
with #688's parameters. Every region with no collision geometry was classified from its
inputs and a rerun, not from its element count:

| Reason (non-open regions) | Before | After | Fill cells before → after |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| cover, nothing fits even at density 1 | 947 | 0 | 23,481 → 0 |
| cover, the density draw declined | 30 | 106 | 2,209 → 2,180 |
| park, nothing fits even at density 1 | 1,358 | 759 | 38,602 → 17,753 |
| park, the density draw declined | 29 | 219 | 2,126 → 5,162 |
| rubble (not adopted) | 1,883 | 1,883 | 14,377 |
| core regions with only their core elements | 16 | 16 | 1,437 |
| `open` (intentional, M25) | 2,553 | 2,553 | unchanged |

No hall, depot, plant, market, compound, block, ruins, checkpoint or hut region was
geometry-empty on the panel. Their `unfilledRegion` exits exist but did not fire. The empty
themed regions were almost all one-tile (6 × 6 cell, about 20 fill cells) cover and park
regions, whose 6-cell lattice has no slot that survives the clear margins and protected
portal routes. Most empty rubble regions are 6 × 3 strips with 5 or 6 fill cells, genuinely
too small. Rubble was left out of this change.

## The fallback

`sitePiecesPerCell` (`map/micro/strategies/scatter.ts`) is the one shared path. It runs only
when the strategy's own lattice placed nothing, and it sites single pieces of the region's
own kind. Cover uses crates, barriers and containers; park uses single trees. It works on the
strategy's canvas, so fill, clear margins and protected routes are unchanged. It visits each
fill cell once in a seeded order, with no retries or recursion, and is skipped above 32
portals. It generalises cover's existing #531 delegation receiver: on all 7,127 cover, park
and rubble panel briefs, the receiver's output is byte-identical before and after, so
`COVER_EXECUTOR_VERSION` is unchanged. No complete-region handoff to another builder was
added (D7).

1,280 regions changed, gaining 1,910 pieces. Every changed region passed an independent
fill-constraint and thorough portal-reach check, and rebuilt identically with reversed
cell and zone order. Every map passed `checkMap` with diagnosis, before and after, with
the same core elements. Placeholder loot in the changed regions fell from 8,061 to 6,228
sites. Per 17.2.11 it was not preserved.

## Effect on navigation

Measured against the before map's own endpoint samples, held fixed:

- Contestant empty-space share fell by 0.2–0.4 points per map (for example 0.944 → 0.942
  and 0.907 → 0.905). Hunter share and dead-end area were essentially unchanged.
- Median detour stayed 1.000. p90 rose slightly (1.000 → 1.011–1.019 at 36 wide), and the
  share of routes above 1.5 did not move.
- 1,831 previously complete pairs now end inside a new piece (`invalid-endpoint`). These were
  old loot and cell-centre samples. None became unreachable, and there were no search misses
  or sweep defects.

So the fallback ends most "generator filled nothing" cases, but it does not deliver #529's
detours or dead ends. Isolated pieces in one-tile regions are not barriers relative to a target.

## Cost and limits

Rebuilding every cover and park brief on the panel took 3.80 s before and 5.27 s after,
averaged over three sequential passes under the machine lock. That is about 0.16 s more
per map.

Remaining gaps: 759 one-tile parks where no 2 × 2 tree fits beside the routes (there is no
smaller park placeable), the rubble strips, and core-only regions, whose post-filters keep
cluster-only cover. Reproduce with `node tools/empty-region-audit.mjs`. The retained
`summary.json` and `examples.json.gz` are in [reports/issue-717](reports/issue-717/), with
pinned source digests, before/after examples, the narrowest filled region and a no-fit witness.
