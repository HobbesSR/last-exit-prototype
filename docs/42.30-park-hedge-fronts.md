# 42.30. Park hedge fronts

Back to [42](42-performance-history.md). Issue #649 under #529, following
[42.29](42.29-park-perturbable-routes.md). The helpers are in [20.10](20.10-portal-cords.md#implemented-helpers-649),
park's policy in [20.3.4](20.3.4-structure-builders.md#park-builder), and Corey's sealing answer in

Content version `types-61`, unchanged: fronts are off unless `hedgeFronts` is set, and the
224-map chain sweep shows no drift.

## Question

42.29 found that letting pieces stand on old routes bends them only slightly: no
portal-to-portal route rose above 1.5 times the straight line. This experiment asks whether
placing barriers *across* the cords does better: hedges that grow inward from the fill's
edge as deep as the cord allows.

## Method

`node tools/park-route-audit.mjs --fronts 1`, 42.29's panel and measures unchanged: the same
three maps (`size-{12,24,36}-1`, #688's parameters), the same focused fixed pairs through every
changed park, the whole-map navigation measure (51.10), the same proofs and the same cost
method. "Before" is today's park; "after" is today's park with `hedgeFronts: 1`, so every
eligible edge start is tried. The retained `summary.json` and `examples.json.gz` (the longest
portal-to-portal detour per map) are in [reports/issue-649](reports/issue-649/), with pinned
source digests at `f02c140`.

Proofs passed on every changed park: `validateFillConstraints`, a thorough
`validatePortalReach`, and a rebuild with reversed cell and zone order. Both maps of each size
passed `checkMap` with diagnosis, and every final spawn → exit and spawn → charger target
completed for both bodies.

Every park's output changed, because fronts replace hedge runs. A park gets no front when no
edge start is more than a cell from a portal, at least six cells from another front facing the
same way, and able to reach three cells; the panel was not broken down by which rule applied. Sealing
happens, but rarely: fronts usually stop short of closing ground off, because their depth is
capped at 55–85% of their reach.

Contestant shares above 1.5 are 0.19%, 3.9% and 2.7%. Every portal pair still completes for
both bodies. The longest detours are 1.75, 2.21 and 2.26, in 24- to 48-portal parks with 16 to
34 fronts. Portal-to-interior pairs moved less (p90 +0.03 to +0.05). Their invalid endpoints rose by
9, 10 and 24 per body: fill-cell centres a front now covers. One hunter target at 36 wide
became unreachable while the contestant still reaches it, so it lies past a squeeze narrower
than a hunter. That is
allowed; a region's interior is its builder's business (20.6).

So placing barriers across the cords does what 42.29's freedom did not: about 3–4% of
portal-to-portal routes now go more than 1.5 times the straight line at 24 and 36 wide, and the
p90 rises by 0.13–0.23. It does not register as dead-end area. Claude's reading, not
measured: fronts make combs and bays whose mouths are as wide as the gaps between fronts, which
51.10's dead-end measure does not count as pockets.
Whole-map empty share hardly moves, because parks are a small share of the map and the
fronts are one cell thick.

## Cost

Building every park brief on each map, three paired passes (ms, before / after):

| Map | Park briefs | Pass 1 | Pass 2 | Pass 3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| size-12-1 | 38 | 1,117 / 1,277 | 1,174 / 1,253 | 1,157 / 1,115 |
| size-24-1 | 134 | 7,633 / 7,061 | 6,816 / 6,807 | 6,836 / 6,852 |
| size-36-1 | 305 | 17,936 / 18,809 | 17,704 / 19,774 | 20,579 / 22,250 |

Fronts cost about the same as the park they replace: −7% to +14% per pass, +5% to +12% at 36
wide. Fronts take ground that later tree commits would have searched. The absolute times are
about three times 42.29's for the same "before" builder (6.1 s at 36 wide), and both sides rose
alike, so the machine was probably contended. The paired ratio is the result; repeat the
absolute times before quoting them. A front costs one probe when its full depth is admitted and
up to about six with bisection. The 36-portal parks spend most of it, as in 42.29.

Many-portal parks needed one tool-limit change: cords winding to every stand of a 36-portal
park passed the witness's default 4,096 accepted path segments, so every front there was
refused as `budget`. Park with fronts allows 16,384 (`maxSegments`, 20.10); the default is
unchanged for every other caller.

## Limits and what is not done

- **Not adopted.** Production parks never set `hedgeFronts`. Adopting it is intentional
  content change with a version bump and sweep recapture (53), and a decision for Corey.
- **Policy is a first guess.** Start order is canonical, so top and left edges claim first
  and go deepest; depth share, spacing and setback are untuned, as #649 asked.
- **Dead ends.** Detour moved; dead-end area did not. Pockets that would register need fronts
  that turn or meet, or seeds that grow into partly closed bays. `growSeed` supports the
  second; park does not use it.
- **Sealing check is by cell.** Ground is filled only when no cell path from a portal reaches
  it. A body needs more than a cell, so narrower closures stay open (conservative).
