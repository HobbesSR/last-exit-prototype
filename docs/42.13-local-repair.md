# 42.13. Local repair, #470 part 2

Status: measured 2026-10-23. Up: [42](42-performance-history.md). Design:
[22.6.3](22.6.3-bot-adoption.md#bot-adoption-stage-4), "Local repair". Follows
[42.10](42.10-sliced-replanning.md).

## Result

Under `content-5` a contestant that learns of a door or ground near itself, or on the next stretch
of its route, repairs the route on the same tick. Under `content-4` it walked on until its next
sliced repath: up to 20 ticks for its turn, then one or two slices.
`tests/bot-discovery.test.js` shows a door seen opening 200 units from a bot rerouting it on the
tick it is seen. Under `content-4`, or with the door further off, the reroute waits for the bot's
next repath.

Across the 13 bot-only matches of 42.8 and 42.10, contestants repaired 548 times, and 8 repairs
(1.5%) found no way through within 500 expansions and fell back on the sliced repath. Escapes
(36 of 39 against 37), stalls (none), budget exhaustion (none) and repath counts are unchanged
within a match's variation. Tick cost shows no consistent difference (below).

**For #425:** reaction to a nearby change no longer waits for a search, and the delay left for
changes further off is the repath schedule (a turn every 20 ticks), not search cost: sliced
searches still finish in one or two slices. A coarse level over the tiles (#470 part 3) would
shorten searches, not that wait, so reaction latency does not call for it either. With the budget
not binding (42.10), neither trigger recorded on #425 holds on these maps. #425 stays deferred
(22.6, "Implementation sequence"); more bots, larger maps or a product need for the middle level
would revive it.

## What was measured

`CONTENTS=content-4,content-5 ROUTES=0 node tools/navigation/adoption-report.mjs`, under the
bench lock, nothing else running, the same maps, seeds and stall definition as 42.8 and 42.10.
Raw data, source hashes and hardware: [reports/navigation-470-part2.json](reports/navigation-470-part2.json).
AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, Node v24.15.0, Windows/x64, the uncommitted change on `fe37e5d`; later edits
changed only comments and dropped a redundant term from the repath check.

`work.route.repairs` counts repairs and `work.route.repairFailed` those that found no way through.
`work.route.repairDeferred` counts repairs put off a tick because less than their 500 expansions was
left of the tick's allowance. That reservation was added in review, after the run above; rerunning
the 13 `content-5` matches with it recorded no deferrals and the same repairs and failures in every
match, so the matches played the same and the figures here stand. So did a further rerun after the
planner stopped counting one expansion past its budget on exhaustion, also from review.
`work.route.waiting` counts bot-ticks on which a contestant walked a route after learning a door
observation or a known tile it had not yet planned with. It includes changes far ahead that the bot
will not reach before its next repath, so it measures how often routes lag what bots know, not how
often that matters.

## Limits

- **Few matches.** The same 13 matches as 42.8 and 42.10.
- **Reach is a constant.** A change counts as near within 192 units of the bot or of its next 384
  units of route, and a repair rejoins 576 units past it. A door that opens a shortcut longer than
  that is found by the sliced repath that follows, not by the repair.
- **Contestants only.** Gladiators plan over the doors as they stand and watch no cells, so they
  have nothing to repair.
