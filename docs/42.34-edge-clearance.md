## Finding

**The main thing that keeps unguaranteed boundary clear is the portal pitch together with
20.6 end caps, not builder strategy.** In the briefs, guarantees and hints come in 3-cell runs,
two to each 6-cell tile side. The common pattern is a hinted half beside a guaranteed half.
At 36 wide, 95% of unguaranteed seam length lies in 3-cell runs. Of those runs, 68% have a
guaranteed run at both ends, and most of the rest have one at one end. Each guaranteed run's
end cap clears one cell on each side of its end, on both owners' sides. That leaves **one
fillable cell in a typical unguaranteed run**. Even with that cell filled, a crossing
opening stays within a cell of it: the portal and its two caps leave five of every six
cells clear.

Builders can therefore reach very little of their unguaranteed edge. Where they can reach it,
builders that use hint walls already fill it, and the other builders leave it clear.

The categories are:

- **cap**: 20.6 end caps beside portals and internal runs. A builder may not fill these cells.
- **strip**: other 20.6 clear cells. These are mostly the side strips of internal runs in open,
  rubble and hut aggregates (see #762 below).
- **empty** and **setback**: fill cells the builder left clear. Only these 28% are within a
  builder's reach.

The upper bound follows from that. If every builder blocked every walkable fill sample on its
unguaranteed edge, the blocked share at 36 wide would rise from 18% to about 41%. Each filled
run would still have an opening within a cell.

Arrival, departure, charging and transit regions are small (42–288 samples). They follow the
depot/market pattern, and the raw report has them.

The builders fall into three groups:

- **Hint walls fill everything they may.** Hall and ruins block all of their walkable fill
  cells: every remaining walkable sample is a cap. Rubble blocks 67% of its hinted
  boundary and 8% of the unhinted remainder (`hint-walls.ts`; 20.3.x).
- **Partial reach.** Cover and park block 9–10% with their own content. Cover's aisle from the
  boundary (`boundaryBoxFilter` beyond 32 portals, the live cord otherwise) and park's
  slot bands are the setbacks. Park's adopted hedge fronts (#760) do reach the fill edge.
- **No reach, though fully hinted.** Market, plant, checkpoint, depot, block and compound block
  0–1% of their own unguaranteed edge, though macro hints all of it. Each keeps an access
  ring or aisle around every piece: depot's complete doorway-wide exterior ring, market's
  bypass ring, the slot band in plant and checkpoint, block's two-cell setback on its
  unhinted sides, and compound's yards. These builders don't read hints at their edge, or read
  them only to choose a lane phase (depot). The one free cell per run is left empty. Hut
  keeps a two-cell exterior access ring, and much of its edge is strip from internal runs.

Hinted and unhinted samples differ only in rubble, hut and open. Elsewhere 100% of the
unguaranteed edge is hinted, so a hint never separates what a builder does there.

At 36 wide, 5,336 of 5,489 runs are 3 cells long. Those runs hold 16,008 units (95%), and runs
of 6 or more cells hold 834 (4.9%). 68% of all runs have a guaranteed run at both ends, 26% at
one end and 6% at neither. A third of units are eligible, with fill cells on both sides

finding still holds and has a cause**: the eligible stretch is the one cell that end caps
leave in each 3-cell run. Only 152 runs contain a unit blocked from both sides.

## Folded-in checks

**#758 step 1 (park bays).** The three #649 examples are measured alone with
`park-bays.mts`. The rendered examples come from one set piece. The hedge fronts are grown to
the park's fill edge. With the region edge closed, 51.10's dead-end measure counts the bays
between fronts: 415, 371 and 941 contestant cells, against 133 without fronts. With an open
two-cell ring around the park, the same fronts make through-corridors. The dead-end area
returns to the 133 cells of the static pocket (#698), and a relaxed threshold (mouths up to
4 cells, area of at least 1 cell²) adds 0.1 cells. **The measure does not miss the bays.
They are bays only where the edge beyond the fronts is closed.** On real maps that edge is
mostly walkable (above), which is why 42.30's dead-end area did not move. 51.10 needs no
note, and nothing changed the measure.

**#762 (inward strips).** `author-inward-grounds.mts --check` passes against all three
authored libraries: 81 variants match, with 1,525, 1,525 and 3,513 references. In the
generated maps, the nine inward families carry almost no internal guaranteed runs. Block,
compound, depot, plant, hall, ruins and checkpoint have none. Market has 2 of 19 regions at
36 wide (33 run cells). Park has 54 of 305 (246 run cells), whose margins clear 674 cells,
2.7% of park cells. Internal runs remain in same-class aggregates of ground tiles: open
(283 of 514 regions, clearing 32% of open cells), rubble, hut, cover and the core-element
singles. These are the strip category above. **No multi-tile set piece needs inward
authoring.** Any detour or empty-space cost is left unmeasured because the area involved is
small.

## What this means for stages 2–4

A builder-side perimeter helper can change the blocked share. On 95% of seam length it cannot
change whether a crossing exists within a cell. The levers that could bend crossings are the
portal pitch (a 3-cell guarantee on most 6-cell tile sides) and the end cap's reach into
hinted runs. Both belong to macro and 20.6, which is #761's territory. The recommendation is
posted on #835.
