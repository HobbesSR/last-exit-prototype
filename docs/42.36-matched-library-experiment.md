# 42.36. Matched passability and the experimental library (#849)

Back to [42](42-performance-history.md). Adopted in [42.37](42.37-matched-adoption.md). Stages 1–3 of #849, run as one experiment with Corey
in the Map Lab on 2026-11-06. The rules are [51.4](51.4-resolution.md) ("Passability") and
Corey's answers in [17.2.2](17.2.2-boundaries-and-prescriptions.md). The authoring lessons are
in [52.1](52.1-perimeter-authoring.md). The earlier baselines are [42.34](42.34-edge-clearance.md)
and [42.35](42.35-seam-pitch-probe.md). Nothing here changes a bundled library, a version
or the fixture. The experimental libraries are `MATCHED_LIBRARIES` in `map/chain.ts`, which
the Map Lab offers as a library source.

**Historical.** #922 made matched passability the only library rule; `MATCHED_LIBRARIES` and the Lab's experimental library source no longer exist.

**Workload.** #688's `size-36-1` (42.23's parameters), macro only (`generateMacro`), one map per
step; fill shares pool `size-36-1` to `-3`. Full builds were run at 12 and 24 wide. The scripts
were throwaway probes over `mapViews`; a retained tool is left to the adoption batch.
"Runs of 6+" is the share of unguaranteed region boundary in straight runs of 6 or more
segments between guaranteed ones (42.34's definition, per boundary run).

The 12-wide baseline for 6b is 3,154 portal length, 5 one-portal regions and 3.9% in runs of 6+.

- **Step 1** makes boundaries more open, not less. The rules alone drop the strips inside
  merged regions (`any` meeting `any` inside one region, 17,718 → 1,029 segments), but the
  default passable for unstated region edges inside tiles opened 4,386 segments in the rubble
  tiles. They were authored before that default, and each meant one 3-cell crossing. `rubble-court`
  could not be placed at all: its cover sides met `clear-rubble-center`'s passable ones. Under
  matching that is now a library error (`setPieceSeamProblems`); the experiment states `any` on
  the centre's edges.
- **Step 3** is the largest single change. Under the chain's old count, cover and park had
  difficulty 0 like `field`, so the authored weights alone set frequency: cover and park were
  about 9% of the fill and `field` 42%. Counting passability, as the old generator did
  (`last_exit_map`'s `getDifficulty`), made them 57%.
- **Step 4** shows how much the weights had compensated. With every weight 1, `field` fell to
  2.4% of the fill: an unconstrained tile wins about 17% of head-to-heads against a fully
  stated one at the default offset of 12. Raising the offset to 36 or 72 shifted the fill
  toward rubble, not `field`, because each allowed rotation is its own option: the three
  four-rotation rubble tiles hold twelve draws to `field`'s one.
- **Step 5** closes rubble regions except at their authored crossings. Rubble then took about
  two-thirds of the fill: four rotations each, and now high difficulty.
- **Step 6**'s corner tiles chain: closed runs reach 24 cells, and a third of the unguaranteed
  boundary is in runs of 6+.
- **Step 7** evens the fill: `clear-rubble-bend` falls from 30% to 11%, and cover, park and
  the corner tiles each take 7–9%. Corey asked for weight to work "the least surprising and
  most uniform way" (17.2.2).
- **6a** brings back the three-segment pattern. The eight themed grounds take about 8% of the
  fill each, all with aligned `hhhPPP` sides and the highest difficulty.
  Corey, viewing 6a in the Lab: "the themed grounds appear to have that cascade of segments
  that I was imagining was happening as expected. Another annoying thing about them being on
  all these set pieces is each set piece then generates a ton of portals which makes internal
  placement awkward. But I think we should deal with that in a future authoring effort."
- **6b** is slow because the `*-inward-*` tiles, fully hinted on up to four sides, score highest
  and clump into large sealed same-class regions (a 32,004-cell hall in one attempt); the
  open-face rule refuses them late, and placement took 16 attempts.

## Set pieces

At step 3, set pieces hold 4,505 of 8,424 slots but only about 27% of region boundary:
1,814 segments between two set-piece slots, 10,167 between a set piece and fill, 32,730
between two fill slots. Beside a set piece, closed-side variants took 33% of cover and park
slots against about 41% elsewhere (37% against about 49% at step 2): a dip in the first ring,
not a cascade. Under matching, a set piece's `hhhPPP` border admits only aligned or `any`
neighbours.

## Builders

Cover and park wall their hinted edges when their class states `perimeterWalls: true`
(`perimeterWallsIfAsked`, `reachPerimeter`, 20.10), which only the experimental libraries set.
At the step 2 configuration, full builds stood 279 edge walls at 12 wide and 910 at 24, every
map checked valid (`checkMap`, no defects), and 24-wide generation went from 22.5 s to 26.6 s.
The live cord passes over any wall that would close a portal pair.

## Not measured

generation cost at later steps. One map per step; the adoption batch owes a seed sweep and
these measures for the configuration Corey picks.
