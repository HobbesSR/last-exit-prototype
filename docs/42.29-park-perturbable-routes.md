# 42.29. Park on a live cord

Back to [42](42-performance-history.md). Issue #750 under #529, following the census in
[42.28](42.28-builder-census.md). Direction: [17.2.11](17.2.11-navigation-structure-direction.md#route-protection-is-too-strong).
Builder mechanics are in [20.3.4](20.3.4-structure-builders.md#park-builder) and the coordinator
in [20.10](20.10-portal-cords.md). Content version `types-61`.

## Change

Park no longer reserves `protectedRoutes` between portals in the empty shape. It keeps only
the portal approach strips (`portalApproaches`, the fixed anchors of 20.10) and places
through `createProspectiveParcels`. Each commit must leave a hunter cord from the first stand
to every portal stand (`discoverCordWitness`), reusing the previous witness where it still
sweeps clear, or the commit is refused and the piece is passed over. No new framework was
added: the coordinator's ordinary `commit` already proved circulation; it now also caches its
fill and access masks. `SitingCanvas` is the type the shared siting helpers
(`sitePiecesPerCell`, `siteParkPocket`) take, so they accept either canvas.

Park was chosen over the census's larger route shares (rubble, cover) because it is
self-contained: cover's `sitedCover` protections are shared with `coverAround`, `coverNear`
and the yard receivers, and rubble's hint walls read the routes too. Park's hedge runs are
also the pieces most likely to cause a detour.

## Method

`node tools/park-route-audit.mjs` (about 15 minutes). One map per size, `size-{12,24,36}-1` from
`generate(seed, params, undefined, 48)` with #688's parameters: 477 park briefs. "Before" rebuilds
the same briefs with the base commit's `park.ts` (`8eb3c58`). The retained `summary.json` and
`examples.json.gz` (the longest portal-to-portal detour per map) are in
[reports/issue-750](reports/issue-750/), with pinned source digests.

- **Detour:** focused fixed pairs only, the same endpoints on both maps (`measurePocketRoutes`):
  the middle stands of every portal pair of each changed park, and its first portal to up to
  four seeded fill-cell centres.
- **Empty share and dead-end area:** the whole-map navigation measure (51.10) on each side.
- **Fill used:** the share of park fill inside obstacles, sampled at 16 points a cell, as in 42.28.
- **Proofs:** every changed region passed `validateFillConstraints`, a thorough
  `validatePortalReach` and a rebuild with reversed cell and zone order. Both maps of each size
  passed `checkMap` with diagnosis, with the same core elements. Final targets were checked on
  the after map's own endpoints.
- **Cost:** builder calls alone, three sequential paired passes under the machine lock, with the
  output checked outside the timer.

## Diagnosis search

The cord witness proves continuous, swept paths found on a free-space mesh, as the thorough
validator does. The grid validator samples half-cell nodes, and it named 1,979 of the 6,154 moved
parks as broken. All 1,979 passed the thorough search. `diagnoseRegion` (map diagnosis and the
sweep's broken promises) now runs `thorough`, which retries only grid misses (20.10). This is an
assumption made in #750, not a choice by Corey.

## Cost

Building every park brief on each map, mean of three paired passes (passes agree within 4%):

| Map | Park briefs | Frozen routes | Live cord |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| size-12-1 | 38 | 0.08 s | 0.30 s |
| size-24-1 | 134 | 0.48 s | 3.0 s |
| size-36-1 | 305 | 0.35 s | 6.1 s |

That is up to 6 s more generation per 36-wide map. It is concentrated: on the sweep's cached
`game` park briefs, the largest 5% (900+ cells, 16–36 portals) take two-thirds of the time.
There, most of it is cord discovery: rebuilding the free-space mesh when a piece meets a cord,
and re-proving paths to every stand of every portal. Two exact savings brought it down from a
first measurement of 12.2 s at 36 wide. The coordinator keeps its witness when new blockers miss
every swept capsule. Discovery re-sweeps only the stands and segments that the added blockers
touch, and edge normalisation indexes endpoints instead of scanning all of them. Park output is
byte-identical with and without them on all 7,047 cached briefs; hut and compound replay with no
drift. Generation cost is once per map, not per tick.

## Reuse by the other builders

What would let the other seven strategies (42.28) move the same way:

- **Siting through `SitingCanvas`.** Any strategy whose siting reads only `fits`, `spaced`,
  `commit`, `fill` and `elements` can take the coordinator as it is, and treat a refused commit
  as "pass over".
- **Protections read outside the canvas.** Cover's `sitedCover` returns its protected shapes to
  `coverAround`, `coverNear` and the delegation receiver. Rubble and ruins pass them to
  `wallHints` as `avoid`. Those readers need the coordinator, or a witness-aware commit, instead
  of the shapes.
- **Pocket access stays frozen.** Park's hedge pocket still commits a fixed portal-to-interior
  capsule. A coordinator terminal set before the pocket is sited would let that cord move too;
  terminals are fixed at coordinator creation today.
- **Cost.** It grows with portal stands and with each mesh rebuild: a commit whose blockers meet a
  cord rebuilds the free-space mesh. Large many-portal regions (depot, market, plant, hall) would
  pay most. Two exact savings are in place (only touched segments are re-proved, and edges are
  indexed). An incremental mesh would be the next one.
