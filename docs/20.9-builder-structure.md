# 20.9. A builder's usual structure

Back to [20](20-micro-generation.md). Related: [20.3](20.3-builders-decomposition.md)
(design guidelines and each builder's mechanics), [20.1](20.1-region-contract.md)
(the contract), [20.8](20.8-placeables.md) (placeables).

Status: guidance, suggested by Corey on #407 (2026-10-22). Nothing here is
checked or required. A builder's one obligation is the portal promise (20.1),
and a builder may follow none of this, part of it, or all of it. It records the
order most builders already work in and which SDK or shared piece serves each
step, so a new builder can address common concerns in common ways rather than
inventing its own.

## The steps

1. **Read the brief and decide whether the shape suits.** Place geometry in
   the reduced fill (`createFillMask`, 20.6). Plan access over the original
   cells plus known-clear context (`createAccessMask`), and site core
   elements in the original cells. Read parameters with their range checked;
   `fraction` in `strategies/scatter.ts` does that for 0–1 values. A shape too
   small for the type goes to `open` (17 M24), through `unfilledRegion`, which
   still offers zone loot.
2. **Protect the promise before placing anything.** Keep portal approaches and
   hunter routes between portals clear, so the promise holds by construction:
   `protectedRoutes` (first-portal or nearby-portal tree), or cached swept
   routes checked as pieces are tried, as `checkpoint` does. A placement rule
   prunes during the solve; don't place everything and then check and retry.
   `portalMargin` and `boundaryBoxFilter` help keep approaches and aisles.
3. **Site the pieces the type is about.** Siting is the builder's; what each
   piece is comes from a placeable (20.8). Prefer hinted stretches for pieces
   that belong at an edge (`boxesAgainstHints`, `hintedCells`; mutual hints
   first through `hintsOfStrength` if the type cares). Record what was handled
   with intent, empty ground included, in an addressed area
   ([20.2](20.2-geometry-access.md#addressed-area)). The production
   `createBuilderCanvas` records intent with each atomic assembly commit;
   `cover` is its first adopter (20.2, #430).
4. **Deal with what is left.** Offer unaddressed components to delegation
   (`prepareDelegation`, 20.7), or close the hinted perimeter with plain walls
   (`wallHints`, #407), passing the step 2 protections and the step 3 pieces
   as `avoid`. Either is optional, and leaving ground empty on purpose is a
   choice too.
5. **Loot last.** Roll zone loot over what geometry left (`cellLoot`, clear of
   every piece), after any spawns a type guarantees (#21).
6. **Return the result.** Elements in the order they were placed, since ids
   come from that order when the region is stamped; a later step's elements go
   after an earlier step's. Fill the manifest with counts that tests and tools
   read.

## Habits that keep output stable

- Draw randomness per decision from the seed, a cell and a named channel
  (`draw`), so adding a step can't shift an earlier step's draws.
- Iterate in canonical cell order, so the brief's cell order never changes
  the output.
- Any change to what a builder builds bumps `REGION_TYPES_VERSION` (53), and
  the sweep baseline is recaptured last, after the report shows no defects
  and maps play acceptably.

## Checking a builder

These are tests' tools, not steps a builder must run: `validatePortalReach`
over the final geometry, across seeds and concave masks;
`validateFillConstraints` for geometry left in a margin; determinism under a
reversed cell order; and the empty-rectangle fill diagnostic (20.3).

## Where the pieces live

`map/micro/sdk.ts` exports the masks, route and shape helpers, hint helpers
including `wallHints`, addressed areas, the production canvas and delegation. `protectedRoutes`,
`unfilledRegion`, `cellLoot` and `fraction` are still in
`strategies/scatter.ts`, shared by the strategies rather than exported
through the SDK. `latticeSlots` in `strategies/lattice.ts` is likewise
internal: the seeded slot lattice `park` and `plant` share (period, phase
channels, first-hint phase, row-major slots over the mask's extent). `cover`
and `ruins` walk their own lattices over owned cells and do not use it.
