# 42.23. Navigation structure baseline (#688)

Measured 2026-10-30 after #690 and #691 merged, with generated content pinned to
`b6768e8` (`types-55`). Definitions and thresholds belong to
[51.10](51.10-measurement.md); the maintained entry point is
`node map/tools/cli.mts navigation MAP.json --out REPORT.json`.

The workload is one authored-library game map per size: `size-12-1` at 12×6,
`size-24-1` at 24×12, and `size-36-1` at 36×18. All use cell size 48, two exits,
eight contestant and three hunter spawn sites, loot chance .04 and tier step .09,
and their matching bundled resolved libraries. This follows the recent authored
composition workload; it is not a seed sweep or a representative player sample.
Full saved maps, raw routes, per-region/type/portal-count breakdowns, normalized
source digests, library/input/output hashes and reproduction commands are in
[the retained report](reports/issue-688/README.md). Measurement changes neither
the map nor generation. No content version or frozen fixture was changed.

Each long across-map row has 24 routes, none above 1.5. Long spawn-to-exit
routes have median 1.014–1.017 and p90 1.015–1.036 (16 routes per map/body).
The raw report keeps gameplay categories and all distance strata separate;
short same-room pairs do not set these long-route percentiles. All measured
routes completed, with zero invalid endpoints, disconnected-target failures,
search misses or swept-path defects. This does not establish complete-map
connectivity: meshes contain 1/1, 2/3 and 2/2 connected components for contestant/
hunter at the three sizes, and sampling does not cover every component.

These results support Corey's observation of fairly straight progress on this
workload. They do not choose a main lever or prove why routes are straight.
The empty-space threshold is half a cell from actual structure; it includes
intentionally empty courts and excludes reserves/claims. Hunters' larger
clearance removes more near-obstacle samples from the denominator, so their
higher empty share is not evidence of different authored geometry. The
dead-end measure is deliberately selective: a width/area/depth-filtered union
of single-mouth mesh pockets, not a count of exploration decisions. Very narrow
or broad mouths and some cyclic mesh representations are outside its definition.

Per-type and portal-count results remain essential when comparing changes:
global percentiles can hide a builder's local effect. Region rows retain actual
portal counts, physical/guaranteed boundary denominators, empty samples and
mesh areas. Route breakdowns identify both endpoint owners; they do not assign
causal credit to every builder along a route. One map per size and bounded
cell-centre/loot sampling do not justify catalogue-wide builder rankings.

For example, the 36×18 contestant rows show how the measures diverge. The final
column is **medium within-region complete routes / detour p90**, with only
three sampled pairs per eligible region. Full distributions for both bodies
and all three sizes, including origin/destination and portal-count groups,
remain in the raw reports.
