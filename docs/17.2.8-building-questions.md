# 17.2.8. M26-M33: buildings

Up: [17.2](17.2-map-questions.md). Load only the related parts needed for the task.

Corey's building library write-up (#185, 2026-10-04) raised M26 to M33. Each
has a working assumption, and [56](56-buildings.md) marks what rests on it.
None blocks the first stage, L1, which changes no output.

26. **M26. A region as an allocation.** #185 calls a region "an allocation of
    space from a higher-level generator rather than a strict construction
    boundary. Builders may place geometry anywhere permitted by their broader
    context." 51 principle 5 says a strategy places geometry only inside its
    own cells, and composition depends on that. Does #185 loosen principle 5,
    or does it mean a building needn't fill its region or follow cell edges?
    *Assumption:* the second. Principle 5 stands.
27. **M27. Names.** #185's "zone" already means a tier zone (52) and a zone
    plan (55), and "span" is close to the kernel's "run". *Assumption:* a
    building's areas are **spaces**; a **span** is a chain of runs, which may
    turn corners, between one space and another or the outside; "zone" isn't
    used for buildings.
28. **M28. What a space means in play.** #185's spaces are architectural:
    lobby, kitchen, bathroom. What should a space say for the game?
    *Assumption:* gameplay properties first: enclosed and roofed (P-11), dead
    end or passage, loot weight, and who can enter. An architectural name is a
    label and a hook for art direction later.
29. **M29. Connections by who can cross.** Should a connection's kind be what
    a body can do: `door` (both roles), `squeeze` (contestants only, 1.5
    cells, P-04), `open`, `window` (sight only) and `wall`? *Assumption:* yes.
    Archways, half walls and railings are the same kinds with another look,
    and wait for kits. Locked doors stay deferred (M23).
30. **M30. What is saved.** A region's result stores its elements, core
    elements and loot (`region-2`, 53). Should it also store the building
    design and its allocation? *Assumption:* not in the first milestone.
    Labels carry provenance and the lab shows the rest. Storing the design
    later bumps the region version.
31. **M31. Feedback upward.** #185 §39 has information "flow back upward":
    "This footprint cannot satisfy the design." *Assumption:* it flows only
    within the region's strategy, which then tries a smaller design or hands
    the region on (M24). Nothing goes back to macro, and a shortfall goes to
    the report (51 stage 8).
32. **M32. What waits.** Building kits need art direction, which the drafted
    types are also waiting for (54). Stairs, roof planes and multiple floors
    need 16's collision planes. *Assumption:* both wait. Until then, a kit is
    a parameter record (wall thickness, window share, door width), and a roof
    is the `encloses` flag on an element.
33. **M33. The first consumer.** Which strategy should get buildings with
    several spaces first: `hut`, which grows larger designs where its region
    allows, or `compound`, whose ring is already rooms? *Assumption:* L1 and
    L2 move the walls of `hut`, `compound` and `depot` onto the library
    without changing output. Then `hut` grows larger designs, and `compound`'s
    ring becomes a design.
