## Independent gameplay evidence

[Gameplay signatures](reports/issue-1153/gameplay-continuity.json) hash every
bot tick (3,557 for seed 9 and 3,103 for seed 4217), plus all 20 scripted
checkpoints. They include runtime players, numeric item identity/allocation and
scatter counters, RNG/event serials, objectives, gates, traps, projectiles,
effects and the complete captured private PhysicsWorld state. Events are
canonicalized to their original English text; public projections are excluded
because their change is intentional.

`tools/relational-evidence.mjs` compares these signatures. Before the outward
switch they were captured with `--out`; after it, `--compare` reproduced all
three exactly. A second comparison ran in an isolated export of pristine
`bab39739`, supplying only the audit tool, pure event formatter and scenario
observer callbacks: it also reproduced all signatures. No simulation source,
arena or fixture was changed in that export. This separates representation
changes from hidden gameplay drift before the intentional fixture update.

To repeat against the adopted implementation:

```powershell
node tools/relational-evidence.mjs --compare docs/reports/issue-1153/gameplay-continuity.json
```

recording without its roster needed migration. Generation, codec selection,
transport adoption and performance benchmarks are outside this change.
