# 42.25. Production park hedge pockets (#698)

This is one bounded production adoption under #529 and the direction in
[17.2.11](17.2.11-navigation-structure-direction.md). It follows the approved
#694/#695 static-barrier witness, but starts from fresh main `63fb8aa`; #695 was
still unmerged at kickoff. Its experiment sources and historical captures are
not dependencies or production fixtures here. The reusable placeable and park
policy are owned by [20.8](20.8-placeables.md) and
[20.3.4](20.3.4-structure-builders.md). Full inputs, raw measurements, preview,
source pins and reproduction instructions are in [the report](reports/issue-698/README.md).

## Delivered capability

An eligible park can build one connected 14 x 14-cell hedge enclosure with one
2.5-cell east mouth. Five touching parts form a single atomic assembly: there
is no clearance ring between its wall parts. Its interior and a two-cell
exterior apron remain free of later trees/hedge runs. The builder retains its
existing protected portal routes and adds a protected full SDK hunter witness
from a portal to the pocket interior. Siting is limited to 2,048 canonical
cell candidates and eight route attempts, with no prospective growth loop.
No fit, no portal, or `hedges: 0` leaves the previous park grammar in place.
This uses existing hedge mechanics and does not establish an authorship theme.

The fixed inputs are the three complete `size-{12,24,36}-1` maps retained by
#688. All real parks are rebuilt, not just the illustrated owner. Of 38, 134
and 305 parks, zero, one and five change respectively. All other owners stay
exact, and all non-admitted park results remain exact. The wider cached sweep
exercises 7,047 parks across 221 maps: 26 admit a pocket across 22 maps, with
zero throws or new portal/fill diagnostic findings. A large 936-cell park in
the 12x6 input still refuses; size alone does not establish suitability.

## Matched movement and structure

For each pocket, nine fixed west-side-to-interior targets exercise the barrier;
three external crossings and three mouth controls distinguish deflection from
entering the pocket. Whole-map meshes permit outside bypasses. Original
gameplay/map-scale endpoints and **all old loot in every changed owner** remain
explicit fixed pairs, including invalid or removed targets. No after endpoint
replaces a before endpoint. Actual after-loot sites are checked separately.

All 108 after-pocket target witnesses complete for the two live bodies, with
ratios above 1.5. Before scatter blocks one contestant and six hunter target
endpoints at size 36; those failures remain in the report. The table uses only
targets complete in both conditions, retaining that denominator explicitly.

| Map / body | Paired complete targets | Before median | After median |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 24x12 contestant | 9 / 9 | 1.000 | 3.842 |
| 24x12 hunter | 9 / 9 | 1.001 | 3.972 |
| 36x18 contestant | 44 / 45 | 1.000 | 3.842 |
| 36x18 hunter | 39 / 45 | 1.000 | 4.274 |

The full after-only medians are 3.842 and 3.972 for contestant/hunter at both
sizes. All after external crossings and mouth controls also complete. Before
size-36 scatter invalidates one contestant/four hunter external crossings and
three hunter mouth controls; they are not silently dropped from raw evidence.
These are swept search witnesses, not shortest-path or exploration guarantees.

Using [51.10](51.10-measurement.md)'s unchanged definitions, each affected owner
gains 132.95 square cells of contestant pocket area and 122.89-122.96 for
hunter, from zero. All six final owners pass independent thorough portal and
fill validation and an SDK hunter route into the pocket. The SDK clearance is
0.925 cells; route measurements use live contestant/hunter clearances 14/25
world units. Those are distinct checks.

All 776 retained gameplay/across-map control pairs at the two changed sizes
complete in both conditions. Long across-map after medians remain 1.019-1.023;
none exceeds 1.5. Physical boundary impassability, ownership, portal counts and
guaranteed boundary structure remain unchanged. Contestant owner empty shares
remain about 98-99%, and hunter shares remain 100%; absolute empty-area
estimates fall while the walkable denominator also shrinks. This local adoption
does not deliver map-scale progression or substantially less empty space.

## Loot and compatibility

The existing `cellLoot` pass runs after final geometry, unchanged. Loot counts
in the affected owners change 411 -> 387 at size 24 and 2,029 -> 1,909 at size
36. Of the old fixed loot pairs, 12 and 53 endpoints respectively are invalid
afterwards for each body. Some other old sites are filtered despite remaining
valid navigation endpoints. Old size-36 hunter pairs also include 70 invalid
before starts/endpoints from the original scatter; no search misses,
unreachable classifications or swept defects occur in these comparisons.
The report retains per-site removal membership and raw failures. This is an
intentional content change, not a requirement to retain every old candidate.

Every actual after-loot site in the six changed owners is reached from its
nearest portal: 387 + 1,909 per body, 4,592 complete witnesses, zero invalid
endpoints, unreachable, search-miss or sweep-defect classifications. Complete-map
composition/proof/core reports also pass. These are bounded sampled-owner
checks, not universal pickup access across every map or an added runtime
loot-reachability framework. Parks still site no core elements.

`park-hedge-pocket-1`; old placeable versions, wire 8, macro 3, library schema 3,

Complete old saved results remain readable. Old layout-only `types-55` saves

owners' elements and loot. Fixed ticks, gameplay RNG streams and IDs outside
the declared generated-content change are unaffected.

## Cost and stopping decision

The report separates three sequential warm before/after family-build pairs
from admission, final proof and offline navigation measurement. Initial
observations are retained separately. On Node 24.15.0 / Ryzen 9 5900X, the final
three pairs over all real parks in each map give these ranges:

| Map / park count | Before family build | After family build | Paired increase |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 12x6 / 38 | 55.6-56.8 ms | 66.4-68.6 ms | 9.8-12.7 ms |
| 24x12 / 134 | 369.1-370.7 ms | 437.6-448.1 ms | 66.9-79.0 ms |
| 36x18 / 305 | 309.2-315.7 ms | 442.9-450.2 ms | 131.8-137.8 ms |

These are measured increases in the park-family slice, not whole-map latency
guarantees. The six admitted sites take 8 or 28 candidate checks and one route
attempt each. Their isolated siting including the existing route preparation
takes 32.9-47.6 ms; final thorough owner/pocket proof takes 1.10-3.17 s and is
not a production placement loop. Offline size-36 fixed-route conditions take
15.6/15.4 s, actual target analysis 11.2 s and structure measurement 47.0 s.
Those analysis costs are not construction latency.

The two-cell apron and protected-route
admission are deliberately conservative. The bounded scan can miss a valid
site beyond its budget, and smaller/fragmented parks often refuse.

**Retain this one-family static composition capability.** It makes local
barrier/pocket mechanics available in production with useful real-map uptake
and explicit access/content compatibility. Stop here. General #649 growth,
other builder adoptions, macro boundary/portal control, density tuning and
broader art direction remain deferred. Much-less-empty-space and broader
progression remain open; reassess those separately under [32](32-delegation.md)
rather than automatically expanding this grammar.
