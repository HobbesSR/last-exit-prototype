## D1. Identifying patches and explaining relinquishment

Answered by Corey, 2026-10-10:

> Let's see. D1 only the builder can identify cell patches it can't fill. When offering cells, it can have a per builder set of tags indicating why the builder is not retaining ownership of those cells, for debug reasons. I'm less concerned about those as being parameters the new sub-region builder utilizes, but might as well have a channel for it.

Recorded decision: only the owning builder identifies patches it cannot fill.
Offers may carry builder-defined reason tags explaining why it relinquishes
ownership. Preserve those tags for debugging and provide a channel to the
receiving child builder. Child builders need not use them; the tags are advisory
metadata, not child configuration or access/ownership obligations. Tag vocabulary
belongs to the offering builder rather than a universal SDK reason taxonomy.
