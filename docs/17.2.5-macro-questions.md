8. **M8. Guarantees inside a region.** Answered during #214 (2026-10-09):
   resolved guarantees on segments inside a merged same-class region are
   retained as internal straight runs and receive the same automatic clear
   margin as other guaranteed segments. They do not become historical portal
   objects and do not acquire external portal minimum-length or portal-pair
   semantics. See the accepted contract in [20.6](20.6-passable-margins.md).
   This supersedes the earlier policy that internal guarantees oblige nothing.
   #313 implemented this: briefs carry internal guaranteed runs (see below).

   The old working assumption was revised 2026-09-29 after the PR #81 review
   found the obligation was stated but never checked: such a guarantee obliged
   nothing. Portals were the only obligation a strategy carried, keeping
   builders to one objective; the Map Lab flagged these prescriptions because
   they promised nothing. The earlier assumption had been that the strategy
   kept them passable. This history is superseded by the accepted answer above.
   Reconsidered during #214 (2026-10-09). Corey:
   "Since there's no real mechanism for keeping track of portals that become internal... although, when I think about it, I don't necessarily see why they can't be tracked, and even have tiles that have passable segement requirements internally to provide a kind of passability guidance... so if there is a clear path to maintaining this maybe we should just do it. If it feels like the only way to do it is come up with a hack or something otherwise kind of inelegent then maybe we don't worry about it."

   Investigation found a clean path. Macro first groups side-adjacent cells of
   the same resolved class, then derives portals; it does not merge previously
   built regions through portals. `ResolvedLayout.segments` already retains
   guaranteed segments inside those groups. Brief derivation can convey their
   maximal straight runs separately from perimeter portals. This preserves
   internal passability requirements for the automatic-margin design without
   historical portal objects or special elision/truncation bookkeeping. External
   portals are recomputed from the final owners and segment guarantees, so
   remaining boundary fragments are grouped and checked in the usual way.
   #313 implements this accepted requirement: briefs carry internal guaranteed
   runs and their automatic clear footprint (20.6, 51.7).
   Internal runs should not silently acquire external portal minimum-length or
   portal-pair semantics. The margin definition in 20.6 gives their physical
   effect.
9. **M9. Does an `open` region need a minimum width?** Every cell and inside
   segment of an `open` region is passable, but a one-cell neck still carries no

   Found in B4 (#97): a passage of exactly 2 cells is too narrow for the SDK's
   sampled route search. `rubble` threw on a 2-cell neck between two merged

   passage at least 3 cells wide (52, "The chain's library"). The assumption

## #214 tile-shape working assumption (2026-10-09)

The issue suggests a centered 4 by 4 feature inside a 6 by 6 clear tile with
`any` perimeter cells. A literal rubble prototype with one-cell borders failed
hunter portal access on all three sampled game maps. A four-cell corner variant
also failed a rubble route after merging with the existing three-cell banks.
Initial proposal: rotated three-cell rubble bands and bends with the unpainted
cells deferred. Corey replied: "I think the question we must answer first is, are
the hunter access checks too conservative?"

A controlled centered-feature witness now distinguishes the two questions:
`tests/map-access-conservatism.test.js` builds a 4 by 4 rubble feature surrounded
by cover. Its 20-cell open ring rejects all 18 hunter portal stands under the
region-local mask, even with no blockers. In the assembled geometry, all 18 stands
are clear and mutually reachable; replaying those routes through actual movement
also succeeds at the same 0.925-cell clearance radius. Thus this local diagnostic
is conservative relative to assembled movement, not evidence that every centered
4 by 4 tile is unplayable. The current independent-region promise (17.2.4 M3)
assumes nothing outside a region. Corey clarified that the proposed automatic
margin is intended to permit denser fills close to portals. Builders need both
the original boundary and portals and the reduced fill boundary, whose new
segments are not guaranteed passable. The corrected direction and remaining
implementation questions are in 17.2.4 M3; no validator or contract change had
been made when this witness was recorded. #313 later implemented the accepted
contract (20.6).
