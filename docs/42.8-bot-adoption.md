# 42.8. Bots plan over free-space tiles, stage 4 (#424)

Status: measured 2026-10-23. Up: [42](42-performance-history.md). Design:
[22.6.3](22.6.3-bot-adoption.md#bot-adoption-stage-4).

## Result

Under `content-3`, bots plan over the free-space tiles: contestants from what they know,
gladiators over the whole map. Across 13 bot-only live-chain matches, `content-3` contestants
never stalled. `content-2`'s grid planner stalled 37 times, a contestant standing still for
5 s or more. `content-3` escaped as many or more at 36 × 18 (6 of 6 against 3 of 6), one more
at 12 × 6 and one fewer at 24 × 12. It opened more doors on two sizes of three. It planned
5 to 14 times less often, and its mean tick cost less at every size. Ticks over the 50 ms budget
fell from 232 to 19. The worst tick is now tick 10, when each gladiator makes its first plan and
builds tiles cold: 114 to 227 ms, against the grid planner's first tick of 46 to 359 ms.

The map worker now builds gladiators' tiles ahead ("Tiles built ahead", below): tick 10 falls to
about 40 ms at 36 × 18, and the benchmark's ticks over budget from 8 to 0 or 1.

These are 13 matches, a handful of seeds per size. They show no stalls, with escapes no worse,
on these maps; they do not establish a completion rate.

## What was measured

`node tools/navigation/adoption-report.mjs test-results/navigation-424.json`, under the bench
lock, nothing else running. Raw data, source hashes and hardware:
[reports/navigation-424.json](reports/navigation-424.json). AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, Node v24.15.0,
Windows/x64. The working tree was #424's uncommitted change on `56bcda2`; the source hashes
identify it.

Seeds 1 to 8 at 12 × 6, 1 to 3 at 24 × 12, 1 to 2 at 36 × 18, live chain params. On each map:

- one whole match per content, every seat a bot, from the same map and seed, to its end;
- the time of each `step`, every repath, every door that opened;
- a stall: an active contestant, not charging, that moves under 40 units in 100 ticks;
- spawn to exit for a contestant, three ways: the unrestricted grid search (`gridRoute`
  over the whole map, then `straightenPath`), the tile planner with everything known, and
  the tile planner knowing only the cells up to halfway along.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12 × 6 | 23 | 82 | 3,378 | 0.996, 0.990–0.998 | 13 / 3 | speculative, 1,362 expansions |
| 24 × 12 | 102 | 273 | 17,902 | 0.984, 0.982–0.993 | 42 / 4 | speculative, 12,772 expansions |
| 36 × 18 | 221 | 692 | 45,769 | 0.995, 0.995–0.995 | 39 / 11 | speculative, 24,341 expansions |

A kink is a turn sharper than ten degrees. Tile routes are any-angle and slightly shorter than
the straightened grid route, with a third or fewer of its kinks: the aliasing stage 3 worried
about does not show at full knowledge. With half the map known, every route was speculative
and its unknown half one straight run, so straightening runs through unknown cells has nothing
to do yet. These cold single queries, building every tile they read, are not what a bot pays:
it plans under a 12,000-expansion budget, over tiles mostly built already.

## Tiles built ahead

Measured 2026-10-23 on the same machine, after the table above. A gladiator's first plan reads
every tile, so the map worker now builds and packs them (22.6, "Bot adoption"), and the room adopts
them; `npm run bench` adopts them the same way, untimed, unless given `--cold`. Three runs each,
sequentially, alternating prebuilt and `--cold`, default `content-3`:

| 12 × 6 | cold | 2.09, 2.07, 2.05 | 3.94, 3.91, 3.86 | 6.7, 6.6, 6.4 | 228, 204, 205 | 1, 1, 1 |
| 36 × 18 | prebuilt | 10.53, 10.39, 10.39 | 15.96, 15.60, 15.58 | 22.5, 21.9, 21.8 | 54, 54, 47 | 1, 1, 0 |
| 36 × 18 | cold | 10.50, 10.54, 10.62 | 15.81, 15.69, 15.91 | 22.1, 21.7, 22.3 | 282, 277, 290 | 8, 8, 8 |

Only the worst ticks move; mean and percentiles are unchanged within run-to-run spread. Through the
real worker, at 36 × 18 seeds 1 and 2, the worst of the first 60 ticks was 44 and 39 ms with adopted
tiles, against 289 and 286 ms cold, both at tick 10.

The cost moves to the worker and memory. Building a gladiator's tiles (seed 1) takes the worker
257 ms at 12 × 6, 972 ms at 24 × 12 and 2,467 ms at 36 × 18, on top of generation (about 12 s at
36 × 18); packed, they are 2.0, 8.4 and 18.2 MB per map, held for the map's life, pooled maps
included. The main thread's longest event-loop delay while a 36 × 18 map arrives was 17 ms with and
without the tiles (three seeds each): they transfer without a copy, and each is unpacked when first
read. Posted as objects instead, they would take 150 to 200 ms to deserialize.

## Limits

- **Few matches.** 13 matches over 13 maps; escape counts differ by one or two either way at
  the two smaller sizes. A completion or speed claim needs more seeds.
- **The first plans were cold.** In the matches above, a gladiator's first plan built the tiles it
  read, so tick 10 cost 114 to 227 ms. Rooms now adopt tiles the map worker built ("Tiles built
  ahead"); maps generated inline, as in this report, still build them on first use.
- **Budget-bound gladiators.** A gladiator patrolling to a far station walks to the place its
  12,000-expansion search got nearest and plans on. No gladiator was seen trapped by this here;
  a hierarchy (the portal graph over the tiles) is the fix if one is.
- **Traps.** Bots still ignore traps in planning, as before.
