# 42.5. Portal graph, stage 1 (#420)

Status: measured 2026-10-21. Up: [42](42-performance-history.md). Design:
[22.6](22.6-hierarchical-navigation.md). No production planner change; bots are untouched.

## Result

The top level of 22.6 works as designed on live chain maps. Across 28 generated
maps, every promised portal pair connected on final collision geometry for both
bodies, with doors closed and openable. Queries cost a few milliseconds where the
unrestricted grid search costs up to a few hundred, and routes are at most 10%
longer than that reference. The cost moves to assembly: region tables take 0.1 to
0.3 s per body at 12 × 6 and about 2.5 to 8 s at 36 × 18.

## What was measured

`node tools/navigation/portal-report.mjs test-results/navigation-420.json`, under the
bench lock, nothing else running. Raw data, source hashes and hardware:
[reports/navigation-420.json](reports/navigation-420.json). AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, Node
v24.15.0, Windows/x64; base revision `1b7b1b0` with the stage's sources uncommitted
(their hashes are in the report).

Seeds 1 to 20 at 12 × 6, 1 to 5 at 24 × 12, 1 to 3 at 36 × 18, with live chain
params and the bundled library. For each map: build the graph (`map/navigation.ts`),
then for each body, region tables with doors closed and openable, then three routes
(spawn to exit, first to last spawn, hunter spawn to exit) with doors openable.
Each route is compared with an unrestricted `gridRoute` over the whole map and
with today's planner (`gridRoute` over the box of both ends plus 600 units).

## Limits

- **The detour benefit is not shown on these pairs.** Today's 600-unit box also found
  every measured route. The benefit is shown by an authored fixture in
  `tests/portal-graph.test.js`: a wall with one gap 2,200 units away, where today's
  box finds nothing and the portal graph routes through the gap.
- The bottom level is the 40-unit grid within each region, so it inherits the grid's
  passage fidelity (42.1). A missed passage would show as a broken pair; none did.
  Stage 2 replaces this level.
- Length ratios are against the grid through tile centres, not continuous optima.
  Routes cross each portal at its middle stand, a likely source of the stretch,
  which is largest on short hunter routes; not separately measured.
- Tables cost about k × area per region, dominated by large regions with many
  portals. Splitting large regions, or a cheaper bottom level, would reduce it. At
  36 × 18 one body's tables cost about a quarter of generation time, both bodies
  about half; at the default 12 × 6, about 0.3 s for both. Delivery from the map
  worker is not built (22.6 stage 4).
- No gameplay, tick or bot measurement: nothing consumes the graph yet.
