# 42.5. Portal graph, stage 1 (#420)

Status: measured 2026-10-21. Up: [42](42-performance-history.md). Design:
[22.6](22.6-hierarchical-navigation.md). No production planner change; bots are untouched.

## Result

The top level of 22.6 works as designed on live chain maps. Across 28 generated
maps, every promised portal pair was **confirmed** for both bodies, with doors
closed and openable: the bottom level connected it over moves that each sweep
clear. Every portal's stands joined its representative. No pair or stand was left
unconfirmed, so no finer check was needed and nothing is attributed to a builder.

Queries cost a few milliseconds where the unrestricted grid search costs up to a few
hundred, and routes are at most 10% longer than that reference. The cost moves to
assembly: with doors openable, the planning case, region tables take about 0.3 s per
body at 12 × 6 and 4 s at 36 × 18.

## What was measured

`node tools/navigation/portal-report.mjs test-results/navigation-420.json`, under the
bench lock, nothing else running. Raw data, source hashes and hardware:
[reports/navigation-420.json](reports/navigation-420.json). AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, Node
v24.15.0, Windows/x64; base revision `a242663` with the endpoint and attachment changes
uncommitted (their hashes are in the report).

Seeds 1 to 20 at 12 × 6, 1 to 5 at 24 × 12, 1 to 3 at 36 × 18, with live chain
params and the bundled library. For each map: build the graph (`map/navigation.ts`);
then for each body and each door state (closed, openable), region tables
(`regionTables`) and stand joins (`standJoins`); then three routes (spawn to exit,
first to last spawn, hunter spawn to exit) with doors openable. Each route is
compared with an unrestricted `gridRoute` over the whole map and with today's
planner (`gridRoute` over the box of both ends plus 600 units).

The bottom level is the 40-unit grid within each region, every move swept with
`sweptClear` at navigation clearance (14 / 25); every portal stand and crossing is
swept to the tile it snaps to at that clearance, and every other endpoint at the
body's collision radius (22.6, "Safety").

## Limits

- **Evidence is one-sided** (22.6, "Safety"). A confirmed pair is a route the body can
  travel. Had a pair been unconfirmed, it would be inconclusive, since the grid can
  miss passages narrower than its sampling (42.1); none was.
- **The detour benefit is not shown on these pairs.** Today's 600-unit box also found
  every measured route. It is shown by an authored fixture in
  `tests/portal-graph.test.js`: a wall with one gap 2,200 units away, where today's
  box finds nothing and the portal graph routes through the gap.
- Length ratios are against the unswept grid through tile centres, not continuous
  optima. Routes cross each portal at its middle stand, a likely source of the
  stretch, which is largest on short hunter routes; not separately measured.
- Tables cost about k × area per region plus a sweep per move, dominated by large
  regions with many portals. At 36 × 18 one body's openable tables cost about a third
  of generation time, both bodies about two thirds; at the default 12 × 6, about
  0.6 s for both. The stand check is a measurement and need not run in a live room.
  Splitting large regions, or the stage-2 navmesh, would reduce the tables' cost.
  Live assembly attaches the graph and the map worker delivers it (22.6,
  "Architecture"); the tables are built only by tools, since no bot uses them yet.
- No gameplay, tick or bot measurement: nothing consumes the graph yet.
